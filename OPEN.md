## 2 · Open questions, in the order they block the writing

Each has a working answer, marked **Working**. A working answer is what the foundation files assume until the author says otherwise. None of them is canon and none is a ruling.

### Blocks Episode 1

**OPEN-01 · How old is he in 2001?**
Canon gives one line and one picture of the real Suris' son: "your son's cake is still to be cut", and a room hung with bunting and balloons (`canon/E01_BEATS.md` B03). The child is never seen.
**Written so in Chapter 1** (one candle on the cake; unaccepted). **Working:** the cake is for his **first birthday**. Born early 2000; twenty-five in early 2025, which is the age O011 gives for the awakening. Aryaman is about four years older (he turns thirty in the season), Purvi a year younger.

**OPEN-02 · What happens to the child on screen in 2001?**
Canon cuts away as his father is seized. The death is not shown and the boy does not appear.
**Written in Chapter 1** (unaccepted): the canon scene as it plays, with his cake and name on the table, his mother's look at the stairs, and his cry after her scream; and one picture under the newsreader's line "taking their places", the man with the moustache lifting him from his cot. **Working:** the canon scene is written exactly as it plays, and the one thing added is what the camera did not turn to: a year-old child in that house. Declared as authored in the chapter footer. Nothing is added that a later episode contradicts.

### Blocks the first arc (Episodes 2–8)

**OPEN-03 · Why did the men keep him alive?**
The house keeps only what it can use. It adopted Aryaman to reach his father's locker (R7) and says in so many words that the useless are removed (Ep 24).
**Working:** he is the cover itself. The men took the Suris' names, house and papers; a family that still has its own child is not asked where the family went. He was one year old and remembers nothing. He believes he is "Parmeet's" son by birth, which Aryaman, who was six, never could.

**OPEN-04 · Why is he gone on the Episode 7 night (O003)?**
The sentence can mean that he leaves that night, or that he is away that night. Either way he is not in the house when the Aroras are killed.
**Working (changed 2026-11-11, after the Episode 7 captions were read whole):** canon has its own door, and he goes out by it. When the inspector arrives at the wedding, "Parmeet" clears the girls of the house out before the truth is spoken: "call the car and take the girls home… Rishabh, take these girls to your house"; and later, "we sent the girls away on purpose" (R17). The sons stay and are told what the family is. Vihaan is sent out with the girls, as the one who takes them. So he has left when the killing begins, by the same order that empties the room in canon, and nothing is invented to remove him. Afterwards he is told what Aryaman is told: the brides were robbers who ran (C, Ep 8 16:01).
**Still his:** whether he is also sent abroad after that night, and for how long. The earlier working answer (sent out of the country that evening) is kept as the alternative.

**OPEN-05 · Whom does Purvi come to love in the first arc?** — **RULED, 2026-11-11 (`O016`, `O017`).**
She comes to love Vihaan. On the wedding night the one she runs to wears his face and stabs her (in canon the face is Aryaman's, because he is the man she loves: R10). He himself has left the house (`O003`, `OPEN-04`).
What the ruling asks of the writing: the love is not announced and not arranged. It comes from who each of them is and from where they stand, in scenes that are about something else (`foundation/RELATIONSHIPS.md` §4). Canon's own feelings are left real: what Purvi feels about Aryaman in Episodes 2–6, her employer and the man the whole office fears, is written as the scenes give it, and what changes, changes by a cause on the page.
**Still to be worked, episode by episode, from each ledger:** where the two actually meet in Episodes 2–6; what Uttara and Bharani see; what Ananta Arora says on the wedding morning when the man is not Aryaman.

### Blocks the second arc onward (Episode 9–)

**OPEN-06 · Aryaman's place.**
The middle of the season is Ahana and Aryaman's love story: the tricked wedding (Ep 13), her confession to herself (Ep 21), the true wedding (Ep 37), the child at the end (Ep 50). O004 gives the heroine's love to Vihaan. What becomes of that road, and of Aryaman, is the largest decision in the project.
**Working:** none yet.
**How it will be answered (`O017`):** he has said that nothing here is his to set up; it is to follow from who the people are. So this is not handed to him as a blank question. When the ledgers of Episodes 9–13 are built from their caption tracks, the road is worked out from what canon gives: what Ahana wants from that house, why her way in runs through Aryaman, where Vihaan stands after the wedding night, and what Aryaman, a decent man who is the scientist's son, would do. The result is laid before him with the lines it rests on, before Episode 9 is written. It is not designed earlier, and not from recaps (`RULES.md` L-08). One thing to read for then: in canon the man she marries by a trick is the man whose face stabbed her, and here that face is Vihaan's (`O016`). Aryaman is not made small in any version (`foundation/RELATIONSHIPS.md` §4).

**OPEN-07 · The curse and the naag.**
Canon's own law, in the cursed family's own words (C, Ep 44 09:10–10:30: "by marrying a naag you must give us back all our powers"; Ep 48 19:56): the Anant kul is freed when the Naagrani marries a **naag** and bears a child. Canon meets it at the last moment by putting naagin blood in Aryaman through Vishakha's bite (Ep 50, 25:52). A man who was born a naag meets it without that. This looks like the reason O011 makes him a naag.
**Confirmed by the author, 2026-11-11 (`O017`):** "accordingly to canon things they are destined to be together". This is the spine. It stays under the story and is never spoken by the narration: neither of them knows it, and for most of the season nobody tells them.

**OPEN-08 · How a naag came to be born to Parmeet and Nimrat Suri.**
**Working:** by the device canon itself uses. Mahadev sends a child that was to be born in one womb to be born in another (Ep 44; Ep 50, 03:57: "I, who was to be born of your womb, had to be born of Pragati's"). A soul of the Shesh line born through a human mother, in the house next door to the one where the Naagrani was hidden. It stays off the page until the awakening. Episode 1 itself gives a second footing: Pragati says she was human in all three of her births and each time born the Sarvashreshth Naagin (C, Ep 1 17:42; `canon/E01_BEATS.md`, second reading).

**OPEN-09 · His true name, and the form he wakes in.**
The record lists the name as unknown, with "Sheshrudra" beside it; the form as not decided. Neither is used on the page.

**OPEN-10 · How much the reader is told in Chapter 1.**
Chapter 1 shows this serial's reader whose son he is: the name on the cake, and the man who lifts him from the cot. Canon's viewer is given the same kind of knowledge about the queen at the end of Episode 1. He himself knows nothing of it.
**Working:** shown, as written. If the author wants it kept from the reader until later, the name comes off the cake and the picture at the cot comes out; the canon scene is untouched either way.
