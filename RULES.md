# RULES — the gate and the pass

**Owns: what "done" means for a chapter of this project.**
These are the author's existing rules, gathered from the author's own files and applied to this show. This file adds no rule of its own. Where a line comes from is given in brackets.

---

## 1 · The pass, one canon episode at a time

```
1. LOAD      read STATUS.md, OPEN.md, foundation/WORLD_STATE.md, the OC file, the previous chapter's footer
2. VERIFY    read the episode's whole caption track and every recap of it; note what neither can show
3. MAP       write the episode's beat ledger in source order (canon/E##_BEATS.md)
4. PLAN      canon scenes are the spine; mark where the OC's thread runs beside them and where it touches
5. WRITE     chapters/
6. AUDIT     re-read as an enemy editor against the gate below; repair before delivery
7. RECORD    every file the chapter moved, in the same pass (list in foundation/FOUNDATION.md, "After every chapter")
```
[StoryOS pipeline, Framework v4.0; The Twin `RULES.md` §2; `storyos-master-skill.md` §6]

One canon episode is one unit of work. An episode is written across several chapters, each made of full scenes. [`storyos-master-skill.md` §5: "3-4+ chapters per episode; full scenes"]

A chapter may not teach canon. If the prose needs a fact no ledger row holds, go back to the source. [The Twin `RULES.md` §2]

## 2 · The gate

| # | Gate | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Episode reference** | The chapter does not open by naming the episode and part it covers |
| 2 | **Canon** | A canon detail has no row in a beat ledger, or contradicts its row |
| 3 | **Order** | Canon beats are reordered, merged or skipped without the footer saying so |
| 4 | **Spine** | The canon scene is not the bulk of the chapter, or the OC becomes its centre |
| 5 | **Ownership** | The OC takes a canon character's action, line or victory; or a canon beat goes differently with no cause on the page and no line under CHANGED in the footer |
| 6 | **Information** | Anyone knows, says or acts on something they have no path to knowing at that point in the broadcast order |
| 7 | **Names** | A name is used before the show has said it, or a name is invented for a canon person |
| 8 | **Staging** | Authored action, looks or dialogue are presented as if canon, without the footer declaring them |
| 9 | **Mechanism** | A rule of how naags, the Naagmani, the dragon or any power works is not taken from the show |
| 10 | **Talent** | The Adaptation Talent speaks, chooses, counts or shows a menu; or anything supernatural appears in him before the awakening (`foundation/ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` §7) |
| 11 | **Scenes** | A scene is summarised instead of played; a chapter opens without place, time, who is present and what they were doing |
| 12 | **Voice** | A character speaks out of their register: the Punjabi of the Arora and Suri elders, Aryaman's clipped half-English sarcasm, the naagins' formal Hindi |
| 13 | **Ending** | The chapter ends on a vague promise instead of a concrete image or line |
| 14 | **Coverage** | A beat of the stretch is neither written, nor marked already written, changed or deferred (a beat may be changed, and a new beat may be added, provided `STORY.md` §1 laws 1 and 3 hold) (`WRITING.md` §4) |
| 15 | **Romance** | It moves by coincidence or by the narrator's say-so; or takes a step that cannot be taken back without the author's word (`foundation/RELATIONSHIPS.md` §4) |
| 16 | **Camera** | A comparison, the past tense or an explained feeling stands in the narration, or a sentence runs past sixty words; or a named feeling is in a place the episode does not show one (`WRITING.md` §2; `tools/camera_check.py`) |
| 17 | **Him** | He is in a scene and nothing is different because he is there; or he is made foolish, or made less than he is, so that a scene can happen (`WRITING.md` §4; `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md`) |

| 18 | **Episode density** | A scene the episode plays is summarised, a thing the episode shows is left out, or the room, the hour or the weather is not on the page; or a sound, a person or an event is added that the episodes do not have (`WRITING.md` §2 law 6) |
**Hard fails, no repair in the same pass:** a fact from memory; a recap treated as the episode; two sources averaged; an invented mechanism; a canon outcome changed for convenience; a character acting on what they do not know.

[The Twin `RULES.md` §1; `storyos-master-skill.md` §2, §3, §5; `AGENT-OPERATING-PROFILE.md` II.3–II.8]

## 4 · What is the author's alone

On 2026-11-11 the author gave leave to proceed on the workshop's own judgement once the foundation and Sara stood and the canon was understood (`O009`). That covers building, reading, planning and drafting. It does not cover what `OPEN.md` §2 lists: his age and birth, the reason he is gone on the Episode 7 night, whom she loves, Aryaman's place, the awakening and its form, any death or reveal that canon does not have. Those are laid before him with the evidence, at the point where they begin to block. A draft is never called accepted until he accepts it. His work is public by his own standing preference, so dated snapshots of this workshop on Drive are shared by link; other people's material (recap texts, stills, caption tracks) is not. Nothing is pushed to GitHub without his word.

## 5 · What failure has already taught this project

| # | Lesson | From |
|---|---|---|
| L-01 | A recap is not the episode. TellyReviews put foxes where the picture has wolves, and walked Purvi into a temple she does not enter. | Episode 1, 2026-11-11 |
| L-02 | A still every ten seconds can lie. A dark frame read as a struggle was men walking up a path. Sample at one or two seconds before describing an action. | same |
| L-03 | Recaps mishear names. One recap has three names for one scientist and two for one mother. Rule the name once, from the episode's own line, and keep the ruling. | R1, R2 |
| L-04 | A note about a line is not the line. "He loved me more than his own real son" was first over-read by a recap, and then under-read by this workshop, which ruled from its own note that "Parmeet" had no son of his own. He has: Ravish. Read the line itself, each time. | Ep 21 10:18; R13, corrected 2026-11-11 |
| L-05 | A summary can invert a sentence. "Had Roma died" became "Roma died". | Ep 21 04:16 |
| L-06 | What is on the workbench one hour is gone the next. Anything needed again lives inside the project folder or on Drive. | `/tmp`, twice |
| L-07 | A door that is shut on one channel may be open on another. Three episodes had "no caption track" until the sister channel was searched. Test the door before reporting it locked. | Ep 37, 43, 44, 2026-11-11 |
| L-08 | The pivot of a design is read in its own words before the design is laid. The wedding night was planned from recaps; its caption track, read whole, changed the working answer and corrected a ruling. | Ep 7, 2026-11-11 |
| L-09 | A chapter can pass every gate and be in the wrong voice. In another of his serials the author had to say it twice: "I'm not talking story but I'm talking about style." The source is measured before the first chapter, and every chapter is put through the camera check. | his Black Clover serial, 2026-10-25; read here 2026-11-11 |
| L-10 | The first attempt at this serial made the chapter his homecoming and left canon as scenery. The chapter is canon's scene; he is a thread beside it. | his StoryOS records |
| L-11 | He is not undersold and he is not made foolish. "you keep underselling him" and "he is not foolish" were said of two other holders of the Talent. This workshop's first module called him ordinary before the awakening; it was rewritten. | his records; `foundation/ADAPTATION_MODULE.md`, 2026-11-11 |
| L-17 | He says "canon style" and I keep hearing "canon story". They are different words. Style is how the show tells: the camera, the register, the density, the feeling, the cut. The story is what happens, and that is ours to grow — divergence, butterfly effects, new events, as many as follow naturally. Fidelity to events is not style, and I must not use it as a hiding place. (`O020`) | his correction, 2026-11-11 |
| L-16 | "Complete canon style" has meant the wrong thing to me once already, and the wrongness is always the same: the story is right and the experience is missing. The test is not whether the chapter is correct but whether a reader who has never seen the show could watch it. Place, weather, objects, faces, and the sound the cue carries. | Chapter 1, his word of 2026-11-11 |
| L-15 | A rule that takes a whole faculty off the page is right only if the page is colder without it. The screen law says no reported interiority, and I made that a total ban: no feeling in the narration at all. The author read the chapter and asked for the emotion, where canon shows it. The cure is not to go back to analysis. It is to name the feeling plainly, once, at the moment the face shows it, and never explain it. (`O019`) | Chapter 1, his correction, 2026-11-11 |
| L-13 | A source in hand is sampled evenly, and the sheets are checked for holes, before it is let go. Episode 1's stills have a hole from 02:30 to 06:58, and the ledger said "the whole running time". On the day the chapter was written the upload could not be fetched again. | Chapter 1, 2026-11-11 |
| L-14 | A plan made at digest depth is tested against the lines before it is written on. The plan had the Suri night and the birth night as one night; the captions, read cue by cue, put a locked house and a missed family between them. | Chapter 1, 2026-11-11; `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` §3 |
| L-12 | An episode is neither squeezed nor dragged. The first attempt compressed episodes. Another serial was stopped at the fifth chapter of its pilot: "What still Frist episode don't end". | his records; `STATUS.md` §5 |
