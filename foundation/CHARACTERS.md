# CHARACTERS — who everyone is

**Owns: every person in the story: who they are, what they want, how they speak, how they end.** Tags as in `FOUNDATION.md`. "Voice" is given only where the episode's own lines have been read; elsewhere it says so, and the voice is taken from the captions before that person is first written.

The last column is what canon does with them. It is knowledge for the workshop, never for the characters, and not a promise that this serial ends the same way.

---

## 1 · Her

**Ananta, Naagrani of the Anant kul.** Born 2001 to Pragati, who died of it. She is the child that was to have been Sushanta's (C, Ep 50 03:57).

| She is called | When | Who she is then |
|---|---|---|
| **Purvi** Arora | Ep 1–8 | The Aroras' younger daughter. Forgetful; wears a calendar round her neck; asthmatic, with an inhaler; layers of socks, jacket and muffler; tripped by boys in the market; wants to be a journalist and is laughed at for it. Nobody looks at her. Becomes Aryaman's assistant (Ep 2). Turns twenty-four. C, TR |
| **Ahana** | Ep 9–38 | The face she comes back in: poised, rich, mocking, a mine-owner's daughter with a trunk of jewels nobody can lift. A killer with a rule: traitors do not get an easy death. She saves the innocent (the trapped miner, the drugged girl, Tina). TR, TB |
| **Apara** | Ep 39–43 | No memory. Bright, modern, kind; lifts a press machine without knowing how. TR |
| **Ananta** | Ep 44–50 | Herself, with all of it. Chooses her clan over her love, then her love over her penance. TB |

- **Wants:** as Purvi, to be seen, and to be a journalist. As Ahana, the deaths of those who killed her family; then, against her will, him. At the end, her clan free and her country safe.
- **Voice (Purvi, C Ep 1):** small, literal, earnest. *"Have you got a cold?" "I am crying."* Calls her sister a genius. Says "you talk as if you will do magic."
- **Voice (Ahana):** from the captions when first written.
- **Canon end:** holds the Naagmani; marries Aryaman; with child six months on.

## 3 · The house that calls itself Suri

Five came: "I, my brother, my sister and my two friends" (C, Ep 7 05:02; R18). **Their children were not told.** On the wedding night a son hears it for the first time: "the truth you did not know till now" (C, Ep 7 05:33; R17). A news empire in Delhi; a dragon under the office; a hidden room of passports, cuttings and weapons behind "Parmeet's" wall (TB, Ep 47). Their rule, said aloud by Dimple: who is no longer useful is removed (TB, Ep 24).

## 4 · Aryaman

**Aryaman Suri**, born Sinha. Son of the scientist Tushar Sinha. Adopted after his father's death and raised by "Parmeet" for the sake of his father's locker (R7). The boy on screen in Episode 1 looks eight to ten (V); the season's "thirty" would make him five or six; prose gives no number. He knows he is adopted and that his father was a great scientist; he keeps the photograph locked in a cupboard so that "Parmeet Dad" will not be hurt (C, Ep 21 10:01–10:18; Ep 22 08:19). He does not know why he was taken. He remembers only that enemy operatives killed his father (TR, Ep 22). He believes he owes the house his life: "I would do anything for my brothers, for my Dad. I would die" (C, Ep 21).

- Star anchor and editor; Journalist of the Year. Drinks all day and makes no mistakes at work. Hates show, crowds and stupidity. Loves his sisters.
- Left on his wedding day by Radhika; calls marriage suicide.
- Honest in his work: refuses a company he holds to be the country's enemy (Ep 16); hunts the enemy state on air.
- **Voice (C, Ep 1):** dry, fast, cutting, never raised. *"Did your wife feed you garlic chutney last night? Shall we report that too?"* *"I am not a pizza, for everyone to like."*
- **Canon end:** learns everything; hands "Parmeet" to the police himself; stands with her against him; marries her.
- He is innocent of the wedding night. The house made him unconscious that day because he "was hard to make understand" (C, Ep 15 11:32); the hand that stabbed Purvi was Bharani's in his shape (R10). Ahana learns this in Ep 17 (C, 04:39).
