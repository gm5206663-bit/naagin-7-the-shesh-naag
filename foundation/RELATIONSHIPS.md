## 4 · The romance (`O004`)

**The rulings:** between the female lead and Vihaan; it comes naturally, among the other things of the story (`O004`). She loves him in the first arc (`O016`). The author does not impose it: "it's not something i put it was naturally should happen"; it follows from who the two truly are, from canon's own law, and from where they stand (`O017`).

**What "naturally" is taken to mean here**
1. It is not announced, planned in the narration, or helped by coincidence. They meet where canon puts them both.
2. It grows from what each one does in front of the other, in scenes that are about something else.
3. Each has a life the other is not in. She has her sister, her forgetting, her wish to be a journalist, and a temple calling her. He has a house he is relearning.
4. Canon's feelings are real and are not erased to make room. If she admires Aryaman, she admires him. What changes, changes by cause.
5. Neither is written as the other's reward.
6. Nothing is declared before the people themselves would know it.
7. The pace is the author's. A step that cannot be taken back (a confession, a kiss, a promise) is not written without his word.
8. **A pull is not love.** That people notice him, that she does, is not trust, forgiveness or consent, and is never written as if it were. He earns her trust by distinct things he does.
9. **Her scenes stay hers:** her grief, her revenge, her wits, her kills. He does not solve them for her.
10. **Her will is shown before any touch.** She chooses, visibly, first.
11. **Aryaman is never made small** so that Vihaan can stand taller. He stays the man the show made him.
12. **Neither of them is told they are meant for each other.** The curse's law (`OPEN-07`) is under the story; it is not a reason either of them has.

(8 to 11 are the author's own rules for his other naag serial and for his Supergirl serial, taken as method: `Sara/UNDERSTANDING.md`.)

**What stands in its way, from canon** (none of this is to be smoothed over)
- She is a naagin and loving a human is forbidden to her (C, Ep 44 04:10); Uttara and Bharani will act against it, and in canon they do it by wearing the loved man's face (R10). They think he is human. So does he.
- He is a Suri. After Episode 7 that is the name of the people who killed her family.
- He was not there that night (`O003`). She does not know it: the one who stabbed her wore his face and spoke as him (`O016`; canon plays it with Aryaman's, C Ep 7 22:34). In canon she believes it of Aryaman until Bharani owns to it in Episode 17 (digest; to be read in the captions when that ledger is built).
- Bharani struck to stop her loving a human (C, Ep 15 02:36). The man she struck for is a naag, and nobody knows it, he least of all.
- Canon gives her marriage to Aryaman (`OPEN-06`), and Aryaman is a good man.

**What speaks for it, from canon**
- The curse lifts when the Naagrani marries a naag (`OPEN-07`).
- His parents were killed by the same men, on their own night, some days before she was born. Neither knows.
