**Rules of the firewall**
- A character moves from **–** to **K** only in a scene on the page, by seeing, hearing or being told.
- The narration may know more than the people, as the show's camera does, and may let the reader see it. It never lends that knowledge to a character.
- Vihaan's column is the one most likely to be cheated. Before any scene of his, read his column.
