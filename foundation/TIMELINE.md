# TIMELINE — dates and order

**Owns: when things happen, in what order, and how old people are.** Tags as in `FOUNDATION.md`: **C** captions, **V** stills, **TR**/**TB** recaps, **A** authored for this serial (not canon; see `OPEN.md`). An untagged date is nowhere in this file.

The show keeps a loose clock. It says "2001" and "2025" on screen, says "twenty-four years" of both, and lets its festivals fall on its air dates. This file records what is said and does not tidy it. Prose uses the show's own words ("twenty-four years ago", "six months later") and never a calendar date the show did not give.
