# VIHAAN SURI — the person

**Owns: who Vihaan is as a man: his place, what he believes, how he speaks, what he must never be, and where he meets canon.** His Talent is in `ADAPTATION_MODULE.md`. The author's rulings about him are in `OPEN.md` and are cited here by row. Everything not carrying an `O` row or a canon tag is this workshop's working design, open to the author's correction.

## 1 · Fixed by the author

- He is the real Suris' own boy from Episode 1, kept by the men who took the name, raised as a son of that house and brother to Aryaman. He is not the sisters' brother. (`O001`)
- He comes home from London in Episode 1, to the family's news business. (`O002`, `O011`)
- He has left on the night of Episode 7. (`O003`)
- He and the heroine come to love each other, and it comes naturally. (`O004`) She loves him in the first arc, and on the Episode 7 night the one who stabs her wears his face. (`O016`) It is not arranged: it follows from who they both are and from where they stand. (`O017`)
- He is a naag of the Shesh line, holds the Adaptation Talent unawakened, and wakes at about twenty-five. (`O011`)
- He is not Sheshveer. (`O012`)

## 3 · Working design (`OPEN-01` to `OPEN-09`)

**Birth.** Born early 2000 to Parmeet and Nimrat Suri. The cake of Episode 1 was for his first birthday. The men came that night. **His name** is the one his own parents gave him: it is on the cake in Chapter 1, and the men kept it with everything else of the Suris. (Working, like the rest of this section; `OPEN-10`.)

**Not the same night.** The first plan here took the Suri house, the scientist and the birth of the Naagrani as one night. The second reading of the captions makes one night hard to hold: by the night of the birth the Suris have been phoned, their house found locked, and the family is called vanished (C, Ep 1 12:30–13:03). So his first birthday falls some days before her birth; how many, the episode does not say. Her twenty-fourth birthday and his twenty-fifth still come close together, at Episodes 6 and 7, which is when canon wakes her and when `O011` wakes him. **Not to be written as fact** beyond what the captions give.

**Kept.** He was the cover. He was a year old and remembers nothing.

**Raised.** As a son by birth. He was told his mother died when he was a baby, which is true. He has been "Parmeet's" son for twenty-four years and has never had a reason to doubt it.

**London.** Sent away young and kept away. The working reason, never said in the house: he notices things. Why he comes back is his own doing, not the house's; the natural door in canon is the surprise Pinky and Nisha arrange for Aryaman's award night (Ep 1, B20).

**What he believes on the day he lands**

| He believes | The truth |
|---|---|
| Parmeet Suri is his father. | The man killed his father. |
| His mother, Nimrat, died when he was small. | She was killed with his father, that night; canon shows neither death. |
| Aryaman is his adopted elder brother, taken in out of kindness. | Aryaman was taken for a locker. |
| The family is Punjabi, from Attari, in the news business. | The name, the town and the business are a skin. |
| He is a man like any other, only luckier than most in his health and his wits. | He is a naag of the Shesh line. |

He holds nothing else. He has not heard of naagins outside stories, of the enemy state as anything but news, of the dragon, the Naagmani, or the Aroras.

## 4 · The man

Twenty-four. He has been away for years, and the house he comes back to is one he half knows.

- **He is striking to look at**, and does not trade on it: lean, strong and quick together, easy in his body. People notice when he comes into a room. That is all it does; it wins him nobody's trust.

- **He watches before he speaks.** He is the one in the room who has noticed who is unhappy.
- **He picks things up:** a language, a camera, the way a person likes their tea. He does not think this is remarkable and neither should the narration.
- **He is easy with people**, where Aryaman is not, and he would rather mend a quarrel than win it. That is also his fault: he lets things go that he should not, and he takes his father's word.
- **He loves his brother** and has spent his life a step behind him without minding.
- **He wants** a place in the family's work that he has earned, and his father's good opinion, which he has never been sure of.
- **He is nobody's fool.** He is quick and he sees a great deal. What he does not see is his own house, because he has loved it since before he could speak and it has lied to him for just as long. For most of the first arc he is wrong about the people he loves, and the cause is love, never dullness.
- **He is not** a saint or a rescuer, and the story does not bend so that he can be right.

**Voice.** Unhurried; asks more than he tells; humour that is kind, where Aryaman's cuts. Years abroad show in an English word reaching in where a Hindi one would do, never in showing off. With his father he is careful. The voice is set in his first scene and then written down here.

**He never**
- solves what canon has someone else solve, or speaks a canon character's line;
- knows a thing before the scene in which he could learn it;
- shows a power before the awakening, or has the narration explain why he is as he is;
- does a foolish thing so that a scene can happen;
- is written so that Aryaman looks small. Aryaman stays the man the show made him;
- mocks Purvi's forgetting, her inhaler or her layers. Others do. He is the one who does not, and it is not pointed out;
- explains himself in speeches;
- is praised by the narration.

## 6 · What the earlier attempt did wrong (`O013`)

Written from reviews. Centred on him. An invented parasite. Invented parents. Episodes squeezed. This file is built the other way round: the canon is laid out first, he is placed where it has room for him, his parents are the ones the episode shows, and nothing here adds a creature, a power or a rule the show does not have.
