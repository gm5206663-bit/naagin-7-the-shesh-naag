## Before any chapter

1. `../STATUS.md` names the job. `../OPEN.md` §2 says what is still his to decide.
2. The episode has a beat ledger made from its **whole** caption track, with stills where a picture matters. A recap is a pointer and never the source.
3. Every beat of the stretch is listed in order, and each ends up written, already written, changed by a stated cause, or deferred to a named chapter.
4. Each person in a scene acts only on what `../foundation/WORLD_STATE.md` §1 says they know.
5. If Vihaan is in a scene, one sentence says what is different because he is there. Otherwise he is not in it.
6. The narration is a camera (`../WRITING.md` §2). `python3 tools/camera_check.py` on the chapter before it is shown.
7. The chapter opens by naming its episode. It stays "not yet accepted" until he accepts it.
8. After the chapter, every file it moved is updated in the same pass (`../foundation/FOUNDATION.md`).

## What I say honestly, every time

- Captions are speech recognition. They have no speaker labels and they mishear names.
- A still is not motion. I have heard no audio.
- What depth each episode was read at (`../canon/episodes.json`).
- A green check is not a canon proof and not his acceptance.
- "Done" only when the whole thing asked for is done.

## Never

1. A chapter he did not ask for.
2. An invention shown as canon. "The source does not say X" used as proof that X is not so.
3. Vihaan weakened to keep a canon scene, made foolish to produce one, undersold, or strengthened without a cause. A chapter that is his homecoming with canon as scenery.
4. Anyone acting on what they have not learned. Anything crossing over from Sheshveer or another project.
5. A fact kept in two places.
6. His sentences pasted back as prose or as rules.
7. A recap essay after delivery; a lecture on the page; prose that defends itself.
8. A known fault delivered.
9. A superseded draft brought back as live.
10. A guess where one question would settle it. And the other side of that: a question about something his own files already answer.
