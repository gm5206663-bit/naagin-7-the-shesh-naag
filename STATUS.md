Said plainly, because it decides what may be written.

- **Caption tracks: 50 of 50.** Episodes 37, 43 and 44 were found on the sister channel on 2026-11-11; those three are different uploads and may be different cuts.
- **Episode 1:** every caption cue, one recap, stills of the episode. No sound heard. The first 23 minutes were read a second time, cue by cue, for Chapter 1. **No still is held between 02:30 and 06:58**, and on 2026-11-11 the official upload could not be fetched again (YouTube asked this machine to sign in), so that stretch of the gathering is staged from the stills on either side.
- **Episode 7 (the wedding night):** the whole caption track, and one recap.
- **Episode 19:** the whole caption track. It has no recap.
- **Episodes 2–6, 8:** one recap each, in full. Caption passages in 3, 5, 6 and 8.
- **Episodes 9–50:** one recap each, in full. Caption lines read wherever a ruling rests on them, including the curse in Episode 44 (`CANON.md` R7–R20).
- **Nothing after Episode 1 has been looked at.** Looks, faces and silent action for Episodes 2–50 rest on recaps, which have been wrong before.

This is enough to see the whole road and to plan. It is not enough to write any episode after the first. Each episode gets its own beat ledger from its whole caption track before its chapters are written (`RULES.md` §1).

**What the check of 2026-11-11 covered.** Every caption citation in the working files was put beside the caption it cites (`tools/cite_check.py`); all hold. The digests of Episodes 2–6 and 8 were checked against their recaps. Every foundation file was read through. **Not covered:** the Episodes 9–50 digests were not re-read against their recaps line by line, and the Episode 1 ledger was checked at its key passages, not cue by cue.
