## The Six O'Clock Call

"Not this one," he says.

"Hold it till I say."

A beat of London silence.

"Vihaan. The window—"

"Correct the line. Send it again."

"I'll sign on the next call."

"Understood. Anything else?"

"Nothing. The house is asleep."

Under it, a folder with no tab lies shut, where it has lived since London.

The call ends.

He caps the pen and sits one minute in the grey light.

Then the house starts below him, and he goes down to meet it.
