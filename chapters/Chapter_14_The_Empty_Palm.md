## The Guest Who Was Not Asked

Morning at the Suri house, and the lawn is a working site.

A man on a ladder with a staple gun. Two men carrying a length of marigold between them. A boy on his knees with a roll of white cloth and a pot of paste, marking out where the chairs will stand. Sheshnaag's hood is not shown, but the lawn feels like a temple that has forgotten its god.

Inside, the front room smells of new paint and coffee, and the elder of the house is standing by the sideboard with his hands behind his back, and the oldest of his cousins is talking at him.

A tray of glasses stands on the side table with a jug beside it, and nobody has poured anything all morning.

Vihaan comes down the stairs with the day's list in his hand and goes along the veranda reading it, smiling small and private at the paper, and the men on the lawn keep their heads down until he has passed.
