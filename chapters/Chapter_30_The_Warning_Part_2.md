## The Confession

Their room holds the last-dark under one low lamp, and the house sleeps under them.

The door opens on her midnight face, and he is awake at the desk with the file shut under his hand.

"Mujhe tumse kuch kehna hai." Vihaan does not get up.

"Baitho."

She sits on the bed's edge, and waits.

"Main ek ladki ko jaanta tha."

He looks at the shut file, not at her.

"Channel ki lobby mein dekha tha use — bulletin mein naam aata tha uska. Purvi."

Her hands go still in her lap.

"Bahut der se samjha main."

His thumb finds the file's edge.

"Ki main use — pyaar karta tha. Aur jab samjha — tab tak duniya ne mujhe uski maut ki khabar de di. Naa — mat bolo kuch. Suno."

The file lies shut on his desk.

"Verma se file mangi maine — missing-person file."

He counts it off like entries.

"Aaj tak javaab nahin aaya. Rishabh ne use chaar-paanch phone kiye — koi response nahin. Staff file mein Ananta Arora ke saamne — sister's column mein — Purvi."

"Apni shaadi ki raat — "

He stops.

"Khuli khidkiyon se — tumhaari aawaaz aayi thi. Gaspe mein ek naam. Ananta."

"Us din se — " His voice drops.

"Kabhi-kabhi — tumhaari aankhon mein — ek saans ke liye — mujhe Purvi dikhti hai."

Her bangles rest still on the folded sheet.

"Vikram tumhaara haath pakadta hai — aur meri saans ruk jaati hai."

He owns it flat.

"Na samjha hoon — na samjhaaonga. Lekin ye raha nahin jaata — andar file karke."

"To sun lo — sab." Vihaan looks at her at last.

"Mujhe nahin pata — main tumse sach mein pyaar karta hoon — ya mujhe tum mein Purvi dikhti hai."

The lamp burns between them, and neither looks away.

"Main yahin hoon."

She says it low, and does not move closer, and does not explain.

"Main yahin hoon, Vihaan."

"Tum — "

he starts —

And stops himself.

The question with an address stands up in the room between them — and he does not ask it.

Not tonight.

The telling stays his; the asking stays held.

He gets up from the desk, and the file stays shut where his hand leaves it.

He sits on the bed's edge beside her — close enough that the sheet dips between them, not close enough to touch — and takes her still hand out of her lap, and holds it flat in his, and looks at it instead of her.

Her fingers open under his thumb, one by one, and do not pull away.

He looks up at her face, and stops a breath from it, and does not close it — and she closes it.

The kiss is brief, and unpractised, and sure.

Her bangles slip once against his wrist and go still.

He rests his forehead against hers for a breath longer than the kiss — and then his mouth drops to the side of her neck, and stays.

Her hand comes up into his hair — and holds him there.

When he lifts his head, his mark is rising on her neck, small and dark.

She touches it once with her fingertips, and does not cover it, and lies down with it showing.

He lies down beside her — her head under his chin, his arm round her — and neither of them moves again.
