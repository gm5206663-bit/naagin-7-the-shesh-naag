# Chapter 31 — The Farishta (Part 2)

*Episode reference: Naagin, season 7, Episode 20, 1 March 2026.*

*Draft; not yet accepted.*

## The Stopping

Night fills the bogie window, and the lamps hum over the frightened rows.

"Ye chal kya raha hai?"

The train's voice shakes down the line.

"Kuch bhi saaf nazar nahin aa raha hai."

"Ji — arey ye kya chal raha hai wahaan par?"

Airul leans into the dead feed.

"Kuch saaf nazar nahin aa raha hai. Mujhe sab apni aankhon se dekhna hai. Camera theek se lagaao. Koi gadbad nahin honi chaahiye."

"Ji, Pasha."

"Camera nahin mil raha hai."

Boots scramble.

"Ae — hat — camera gir gaya kaheen — ae — "

"Mil gaya."

The camera swings on its fallen strap.

On the train, the men brace.

"Koi nahin hilega — koi bhi nahin. Anantnag station bas kuch hi minaton mein aane ko hai."

"Ji bhai. Hamaara mission poora hi hone waala hai."

"To phir koodne ki taiyaari karte hain."

The leader checks his strap.

"Ji bhai — full taiyaar hain hum koodne ke liye. Ae chal."

"Hey Shivji — hamaari raksha karo."

The passengers fold their hands in the rocking dark.

"Dhamaka — dhamaka hone waala hai. Train ke Anantnag pahunchte hi bomb phatega — aur sabke chithde phat jaayenge."

"Har Har Mahadev."

The chant rises.

"Har Har Mahadev. Har Har Mahadev."

Faith — louder than the guns.

"Chup."

The gun swings.

"Koi bhagwaan nahin bacha paayega aaj tumhein. Ye mat socho ki tumhaara bhagwaan bacha lega. Tab maroge tum."

Far away, in a room gone dark with the whole city, a man counts down the last seconds on his fingers.

Ten.

Nine.

Eight.

A phone glows and dies in the dark.

"Ye koi laga nahin — no connection."

Aryaman slams the dead board.

"Can somebody please turn on the goddam generator?"

"Sir — hum try kar rahe hain."

The junior's voice cracks.

"Lekin poori Delhi ki light gayi hai."

"Chalo — chalo — hoshiyaari bilkul nahin. Chalo."

The men push the passengers down the aisle.

"Hey bhagwaan — bachaao — hey Bholenath — raksha karo — "

"Andar chalo."

The chant breaks and re-forms.

"Bholenath raksha karo — Bholenath bacha lo — bhagwaan — Bholenath — "

Under the prayers, the score comes up.

The seconds fall like stones in the dark.

"Gun — gun kahaan gayi?"

A man stares at his empty hands.

"Ye kya ho raha hai? Arey ye kya ho raha hai — bhai kuch bhi dikhaai nahin de raha. Ye sab kya ho raha hai?"

Under it, the sting — one knock, and gone.

"Meri calculation kabhi galat ho hi nahin sakti."

Airul stares at the silent feed.

"Phone — mera phone — "

## The Fight

Smoke crawls along the bogie floor, and the lamps burn thin through it.

"Arey kahaan mar gaye tum sab log?"

The leader spins in the smoke.

"Sab kahaan gaye? Kidhar gaye saare log? Koi bhi dikhaai nahin de raha. Ye — arey ye kya ho raha hai? Aur kya ho raha hai? Idhar — kaun ho tum?"

She steps out of the smoke, and the train holds its breath.

"Sabse pehle to ek Bharatiya — aur uske baad ek Naagin."

Her voice fills the bogie.

"Naagin hoon main. Naagin."

Under it, the been takes up its phrase.

The men stare.

Someone's gun clatters to the floor.

"Tum log chaahte the na — ki ye train us jagah takra jaaye."

She moves down the aisle, and they fall back from her.

"To phir — bilkul waisa hi hoga."

The fight is short.

It is not a fight the men will ever describe the same way twice — a whirl of smoke, a flash of scale-gleam, the RAW officer rising from his seat with a broken strap in his fist and taking his first clean swing beside her.

The train screams faster into the dark.

The doors hold fast — every door locked, every handle dead.

A camera lies dead among the seats.

"Bhagwaan ka laakh-laakh shukr hai — hum log bach gaye."

The passengers clutch each other in the sudden stillness.

"Pata nahin wo kaun farishta tha — jisne hum sabko bacha liya."

Awe — and the score comes up soft under it.

"Mera camera — camera mein bhi kuch record nahin hua hoga."

Roma stares at the dead lens.

"Lekin wo kaun tha?"

She is already gone from the train's side and into the dark when the night splits open with fire behind them, past the emptied track.

## Ananta

The small station breathes quiet after the fire.

Anantnag.

A lamp burns over the empty platform.

An old man with ash on his brow looks up as she passes.

"Tum kaun ho, beti?"

"Ananta."

She stops.

"Main jis jagah se aati hoon — us jagah ka naam Anantnag hai."

Her feet stir the dust by the door.

He smiles.

"Is jagah ka naam bhi Anantnag hai. Yaani hum dono is cheez mein ek jaise hi hain."

"Nahin, baba."

Her voice softens, but it does not bend.

"Hum dono mein bas ek hi cheez ek jaisi hai. Ki hum dono Shiv bhakt hain."

Under it, one low note holds.

Tenderness — but it does not bend.

He bows his head to her, and she walks on into the dark.

## The News

The lamp holds over the office desk, and the floor leans in.

"Roma — tum theek ho na?"

Aryaman grips the phone with both hands.

"Are you sure? Okay."

The phone clicks back into its cradle.

"Train crash ho chuki hai."

He looks up, and his grin splits the grime on his face.

"Lekin saare passengers ko bacha liya gaya hai. Aur saare ke saare terrorist maare gaye hain."

Under it, the score comes up.

The room erupts.

Dinesh sits down hard, laughing and shaking at once.

Joy — loud, and shaking.

Vihaan comes through the door with the night still on him.

"Dinesh — paani." Vihaan crosses to him through the cheering and presses the glass into his hand.

"Peeyo. Aaraam se."

A glass stands empty by Dinesh's elbow.

Dinesh glances at Vihaan, and drinks.

In another house, the same news lands like a stone.

"Bhai sahab — ye Aryaman kya bol raha hai? Plan fail ho gaya — matlab Airul humein zinda nahin chhodega. Jaan se maar dega."

## The Venom

The corridor lamp burns outside the half-open door.

"Arey main — I am sorry."

Aryaman stops in front of Radhika, and the apology curdles in his mouth.

"But I hate you — main tumse nafrat karta hoon."

Radhika goes still.

"Tumne jo mere saath kiya hai — tumne jaise mujhe is duniya ki har aurat se nafrat karne ki wajah di hai — wo main kabhi bhool nahin paaoonga."

His voice shakes.

"Main chaahkar bhi tumhein kabhi maaf nahin kar paaoonga."

"Radhika — dikhaao."

She lifts a hand.

"I am okay. Stop."

A file lies forgotten on the bench.

At the corridor's edge, half past the door, Ahana stops walking.

"Sir — breaking news telecast karni hai."

Dinesh bursts in, breathless.

"Aapko abhi ke abhi chalna hoga — aur phir se bulletin shuru karna hoga."

Under it, the score comes up.

Aryaman stares at Radhika one beat longer.

Then he turns on his heel and goes.

The venom stays behind him in the corridor.

## The Ointment

The corridor empties under the humming lamp.

"Ruko."

He crosses to her in three strides.

"Ye — ye kya hai?"

It runs thin from her sleeve's edge, dark against her wrist.

His hand closes around the doorframe.

"Kuch nahin."

She tries to pull the sleeve down.

"Main theek hoon."

The keys ring softly as he kneels.

"Baithe raho." Vihaan is already back with the first-aid box, kneeling to her level, and his hands shake once before they steady.

"Dikhaao. Dheere se."

Fear — one breath of it.

She watches him work — the cotton, the careful fingers, the ointment spread thin over the torn skin.

"Ye kaise hua?"

He does not look up.

"Washroom mein — "

Her voice stays level.

"Phisal gayi thi."

His hands go still for one breath.

Then they move again, finishing the dressing, tying the bandage with a knot too neat for the shaking underneath.

His mark shows over her collar in the lamplight — hours old, and his — and he looks away from it first.

"Tum ye ointment lagaao."

He presses the tube into her palm and closes her fingers over it.

"Achhe se lagaana hai."

He believes the washroom the way a man believes a locked door — he does not push it, and he does not leave it.

He files it shut beside everything else he carries for her, and says nothing of the filing.

"Kisi se inki dressing karvaao jaldi." He tells the passing junior without looking away from her.

"Okay, sir."

## The Bulletin

The studio screen throws its light over the crowded floor.

"Aatanki hamla hua naakaam."

The sting rolls across the dark city.

"Amarnath Express ke saare yaatri mehfooz. Aatankvaadiyon ko maar giraaya gaya."

The office floor crowds around the one working screen.

"To doston — jeet hamaari hui."

Aryaman faces the camera, and the city leans in.

"Aur Changistan ek baar phir haar gaya. Sahi suna aapne. Humein hamaari sources se pata chala hai — ki is attack ke peeche hamaara padosi mulk Changistan tha. Aur is aatanki hamle mein Changistan ko ek baar phir ek karaari maat mili hai."

Under it, the score comes up.

Dinesh fumbles the recording deck, fingers slipping.

Vihaan steps in beside him, steadies the deck with one hand, and presses record with the other.

The tape turns inside the recording deck.

"Lekin friends — humein hamaari apni sources se ye bhi pata chal chuka hai. Ab bhi is desh mein kayin Changistani maujood hain — jo bhesh badal kar aapke aur hamaare beech khud hamaare apne ban kar reh rahe hain."

Aryaman's voice hardens.

"Ye Changistan ke log ghatiya hote hain, bahut zyada. I hate them."

The floor murmurs.

"Haan. Main to kehta hoon — kill them more. Maar daalo sabko."

Aryaman leans into the lens.

"Yes — we win. Ye vaada hai hamaara. Desh ke gaddaar chaahe kitne ho — kaheen bhi ho — wo chaahe hamaare kitne sage bankar hamaare beech reh rahe hon. Hum unhein chhodenge nahin. Chun chun kar maarenge. This we promise you."

Triumph — loud and righteous.

Dinesh's pen rolls off the console.

"Ye tape subah ki file mein jaayegi." Vihaan checks the deck's red eye, and nods to Dinesh.

"Sambhaal ke."

In another house, the same promise lands like a sentence.

"Bhai sahab — taiyaar ho jaaiye. Aryaman to sirf keh raha hai. Airul to humein jaan se maar dega."

## The Doubt

The flame moves over the den wall, and the jewel drinks it in.

"Ye ho nahin sakta."

Airul stares at the jewel in his palm.

"Arey Naagmani to hamaare paas hai. Uske baavajood — Naagmani mein wo shakti hai — wo roohaani shakti hai — jis se aadmi kuch bhi paa sakta hai. Uske baad bhi — uske baad bhi nahin — ye ho nahin sakta. Kuch sahi nahin hai."

Under it, low strings.

He turns the stone over.

It gives him nothing back.

Doubt takes hold.

The cup stands untouched by his elbow.

"Kaheen iska matlab ye to nahin — ki ye Naagmani nakli hai."

The whisper fills the room.

"Agar ye nakli hai — to iska pata chal jaayega. Kyonki poornima ki raat ko — ye Naagmani apna rang zaroor dikhaayegi."

His fist closes over the jewel.

A list lies open on the low table.

"Lekin mere aaj ke khel ka rang kisne bigaad diya."

His voice turns to iron.

"Ye jaanna zaroori hai — ki hamaara dushman hai kaun."

Elsewhere, two voices bend close in the dark.

"Train pe jo itna bada hamla hua — wo sirf desh mein dahshat phailaane ke liye nahin — balki us RAW officer ko jaan se maarne ke liye bhi hua tha."

Ahana keeps her voice low.

"Aapko jo khat mila hai — us hisaab se wo chhah log kuch aisa kar rahe hain — jo desh ko kisi badi museebat se bachaayega. Lekin usmein se ek to mar chuka hai."

Bharani listens without moving.

"Humein ye jaanna zaroori hai — ki paanch aisa kya kar rahe hain, Bharani — jise Changistani rokna chaahte hain."

She straightens.

"Wo to unse baat karke hi pata chalega. Aur uske liye humein unse milna hoga. Ye jaanne ke liye — ki aakhir unka plan hai kya."

## The Den

The den lamps burn low over the stone floor.

"Hamaara plan fail kaise ho gaya?"

Airul's roar shakes the den.

"Arey wo train kaise bach gayi? Hamaare haathon se ye jeet kaise chali gayi? Laanat hai. Wo sala RAW ka kutta bhi bach gaya."

Parmeet and Baljeet press back against the wall.

"Sirf bees din bache hain — itne bade hamle ke liye."

Airul bears down on them.

"Ye mera mission tum sab ne barbaad kar diya hai. Kaise bataao? Arey ye sab hua kaise? Train ke andar hua — andar hi to koi tha — jo koi na koi yaqeenan hai wahaan par — jisne unhein bachaaya hai."

His glass shatters against the wall.

"Log use farishta keh rahe hain."

Baljeet's whisper shakes.

"Lekin — "

"Lekin ye main jaanta hoon — aisa kaam sirf wahi kar sakti hai."

Airul's eyes burn.

"Anant kul ki Naagrani."

Silence.

The brothers do not breathe.

"Lekin tum logon ne to use maar diya tha na."

He turns on them slowly.

"Kyon? Sahi keh raha hoon na main?"

"Haan — humaara — hum nahin maara tha."

Parmeet stammers.

"Hum nahin maara tha. Hum nahin maara tha."

The list of names lies torn on the table.

"To phir kisne bachaaya unhein?"

Airul's voice drops to a blade's edge.

"Kaun tha wo farishta — jo beech mein aaya?"

"Ye log milkar kuch na kuch bana rahe hain."

He paces, thinking aloud.

"Kuch aisa — jo meri poori planning ko mitti mein milaa de sakta hai. Ek to pehle hi mar chuka hai — aur mujhe poora yakeen tha ki tum sab ne hi use maara hai. Kyon, bataao? Maara hai na use?"

"Humne hi maara."

The brothers trip over each other.

"Humne hi maara. Humne maara. Humne maara — humne maara."

"Aapne chhah naam diye — usmein se paanch ab bache hain."

Parmeet wrings his hands.

"Firing kar diye. Khatm kar diye. Pasha — finish."

"Mujhe ye lagta hai — ki ye kisi aur ke haath ka kaam hai."

Airul stops pacing.

"Baba aa gaya."

The door opens, and Tiger pads in on silent feet.

"Mere pyaare bachche — koi na koi hai jo hamaari madad ko aa raha hai."

Airul strokes the great head.

"Aur wo — jaao, use lekar aao. Main khud dekhna chaahta hoon — wo mara pada dragon zinda hai ke nahin. Hai na, chhote? Ye kaam to bas wahi dragon kar sakta hai."

He looks up, and his smile vanishes.

"Aur sochne waali baat to ye hai — agar tumhaari di gayi ye Naagmani asli hai — to phir wo train pe attack ruk kaise gaya?"

The jewel glints in his fist.

"Kaheen is Naagmani mein koi khot nahin hai. Koi khot nahin hai. Ekdam asli hai. Ekdam asli hai."

"Poornima faisla karegi."

His voice fills the den.

"Pata chal jaayega — ki ye Naagmani asli hai — ya phir tum dono ne mujhe dhokha diya hai."

The brothers freeze.

Terror has them by the throat.

"Aur agar tumse koi gustaakhi hui hai — to ye hamaara bachcha tumhaara gosht noch-noch ke kha jaayega."

Tiger's growl rolls under the words.

"Chhithde chhithde kar dega tumhaare."

"Bhai sahab — Naagmani sachmuch mein asli hai na?"

Baljeet whispers.

"Arey asli hai — asli hai. Chup kar — asli hai."

Under it, the score comes up — low.

## The Vow

Night lies heavy over the quiet room, and one lamp burns between them.

"Wo Naagmani nakli hai."

Bharani says it flat, in the dark where only she hears.

"Kaheen unhein is baat ka pata na chal jaaye. Kyonki agar Naagmani asli hoti na — to phir unki soch naakaam nahin hoti."

"Mujhe bas is baat ka darr hai — ke agar unhein ye baat pata chal gayi — ki wo Naagmani nahin — balki bas koi samudri ratna hai na — to aap bahut sangin dikkat mein aa jaayengi."

Her ring turns on her finger as she listens.

Ahana listens with her arms folded tight.

"Duniya ki nazron mein bhale hi — "

Bharani's voice tangles, and straightens.

"Lekin hum to asal sach jaante hain na."

She turns away.

"Duniya ke liye main Dawan ki beti hoon. Suri khaandaan ki bahu. Sab — sirf isliye — "

"Agar usne shaadi nahin ki — to usne use chhoda kyon?"

The question escapes her sideways.

"Kya?"

Bharani turns.

"Kisne chhoda? Kya bol rahi hain aap?"

"Radhika ne Aryaman ko."

Ahana faces her.

"Main yahaan desh aur dharm sankat ki baatein kar rahi hoon — "

Bharani spreads her hands.

"Aur aap apne pyaar ke khayaalon mein khoi hui hain."

"Nahin — wo — "

Ahana starts.

"Bharani — actually main bas yahi soch rahi thi — ki mera ye jaanna zaroori hai — ki Radhika ne agar kisi aur se shaadi nahin ki — to usne Aryaman se jhooth kyon bola? Usne usse shaadi kyon nahin ki?"

"Uttama sahi kehti thi."

Ahana's voice goes thin.

"Waise bhi Uttama ki aatma mujhe kos rahi hogi. Pata hai — agar abhi wo zinda hoti na — to mujhe jaan se maar deti. Kyonki jo main ye kar rahi hoon — use vaada-khilaafi kehte hain. Ek Naagin hoke — aapko insaanon ki duniya mein kheench rahi hoon. Par kya karoon. Majboor hoon."

A cup cools untouched between them.

"Jaanti hoon main ki jab tak aapko ye baat nahin pata chalegi — ki Radhika ne us Aryaman se shaadi kyon nahin ki — "

Bharani steadies.

"Jab tak aapko apne sab sawaalon ke jawaab nahin milenge — aur aapka dhyaan desh par nahin jaayega."

"Haan — aur tum bhi ye baat jaan lo, Bharani."

Ahana's voice hardens.

"Ki mera ye jaanna isliye zaroori hai — kyonki meri shaadi us Vihaan se ho gayi hai — "

Her fingers find the pendant at her nape — his pendant, clasped there two nights past — and hold it.

"— aur main usse sirf nafrat karti hoon. Bas nafrat."

"Wo to dikh raha hai."

Bharani looks at her hand on the pendant, and her voice gentles despite herself.

"Par ek vaada aapko mujhse karna padega. Jaise hi aapko ye pata chal jaayega — ki Radhika ne Aryaman se shaadi kyon nahin ki — aap apni saari ki saari uljhanein chhod ke mere paas aayengi — aur meri uljhan suljhaane mein meri madad karengi."

Under it, the score comes up.

The room holds its breath between them.

"Ismein koi shak nahin hai, Bharani."

Her hand drops from the pendant, but slowly.

"Desh mere liye pehle hai. Mujhe Radhika aur Aryaman ke baare mein isliye bhi jaanna zaroori hai — kyonki wo mera kaam bhi hai."

"Tum — tum samajh rahi ho na main kya keh rahi hoon."

The score comes up under it — full, and final.

Resolve — spoken aloud, and witnessed.

"Bilkul."

Bharani bows her head.

"Ye sab aap isliye to kar rahi hain — kyonki ye aapka kaam hai. Dil ki baat thodi na hai."
