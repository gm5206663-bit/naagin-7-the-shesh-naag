# The canon-placement audit — 2026-11-11

**Owns: the record of the placement audit. Every room, every occupant, every speaker, Chapters 1 to 8.**

*Commissioned by the author after he caught the basement in Chapter 8: "I notice mistakes like this problem before so please correct them, i want you to full check."*

## What was being hunted

Not the wording. `canon/STYLE_AUDIT.md` had already checked the wording, and the wording held: the Hindi is the track's, transliterated cue for cue. The Chapter 8 defect was of a different kind.

> A line can be word-for-word canon and still be staged in a room canon never put it in, with people canon never put there, hearing what canon never let them hear.

`canon/sources/tellyreviews/E03_2026-01-03_tellyreviews.md` line 32 says Parmeet *takes his friends to the basement to reveal the dragon*. The chapter had the same words coming up a stairwell shaft, overheard by Purvi and Mayuri. The words were right; the room was invented; **the room is canon too.**

So this audit asked four questions of every scene in Chapters 1 to 8:

1. **Place.** Is this scene in the room the episode puts it in?
2. **Occupants.** Are the people in it the people the episode puts in it — and nobody else?
3. **Speaker.** Does each canon line come out of the mouth canon gives it to?
4. **Knowledge.** Does anybody in the scene learn something the episode does not let them learn?

The authorities, in order: `canon/E01_BEATS.md`, `canon/E02_BEATS.md`, `canon/E03_BEATS.md` (place, occupants and timecode), the three TellyReviews recaps (room-level facts), and `captions/E01–E09.hi.txt` (the words, and who is near whom when they are said).

## The finding, in one line

**Chapter 8 was not the only chapter with a room of its own.** Sixteen defects were found across Chapters 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 — four of them the same shape as the basement: canon's words, or canon's slot, in the wrong hands.

**What the ripples say about Chapter 9.** B29 is *the lift that leads down*, and B32 is Uttara telling her *you are becoming a naagin*. - **Whether Purvi is inside the temple in Episode 1.** She is not, and Chapter 3 keeps her out.
