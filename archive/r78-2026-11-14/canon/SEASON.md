# SEASON — every episode, in order

**Owns: what happens in each canon episode, at digest depth.** One entry per episode. Each entry names what it was read from. A digest is for understanding the whole road; it is **not** a beat ledger. Before an episode is written, it gets its own ledger from its full caption track (`canon/E##_BEATS.md`).

Witness tags: **C** captions (the episode's own speech), **V** stills, **TR** TellyReviews recap, **TB** TellyBoosters recap. Recaps have been wrong on pictures and on names; anything tagged only TR or TB is provisional.

A note on her names. The heroine is born **Ananta**, Naagrani of the Anant kul. The Aroras raise her as **Purvi**. After Episode 8 she lives in the Suri house as **Ahana**. The recaps use all three, and sometimes "Ananta" for her and sometimes for her dead sister. Each entry below says which is meant.
