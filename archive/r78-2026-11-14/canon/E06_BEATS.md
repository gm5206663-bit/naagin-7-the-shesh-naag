## §2 · The rulings, made before the prose

1. **"Ramesh" is Ravish.** The track uses *Ramesh bhai* twice, once in the introduction and once inside the file. One man, one name; the ledger records the garble and no character in this book is called Ramesh.
2. **The elder is the one who has staked everything.** B01's *"mera bahut kuch daav par laga hai"* is read as the head of the house speaking to his own family about why the bride's family is seated at his table. He is not named in this episode; he is **Parmeet** in this workshop, and the sentence is his.
3. **The bride's father is Gurinder Arora** and the small talk of B02 is his, at Attari and the small Shivling. This is the season's largest coincidence and canon plays it as politeness.
4. **The phone in B24 was taken off a table by the cousins.** The track does not say whose joke it is; the joke needs a phone that is not its owner's, and the cousins are the only people in that room with both the motive and the reach. Written as a prank with a stolen phone.
5. **The unwell woman of B23 is Dimple**, the woman of that house. Canon gives the cancelled guests a different explanation in B34, and the two things are not the same sickness; the page does not join them.
6. **Purvi's birthday is this night.** B27 says it, B32 gives it a cake, and B38 dates the Mahanaagrani's twenty-fourth birthday to the same hours. This chapter is the clock arriving.
7. **The gold kechuli is canon's own identification** (B26) and is the same rule the workshop carries: no naag's kechuli is gold but the born Naagrani's.
8. **Vihaan is given four touches and no canon line**, declared in the chapter's footer.
