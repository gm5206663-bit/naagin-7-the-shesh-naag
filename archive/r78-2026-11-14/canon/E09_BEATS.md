# Episode 9 — the beats, read from the caption track

**Owns: Episode 9's ledger (37:45), cut from `captions/E09.hi.txt` on 2026-11-12, before any prose — and the rulings that keep the
naag material inside `canon/SUPERNATURAL.md`, because this is the first episode of the six-month leap and the first one with a
naagin living in the Suri house under a name that is not hers.**

Episode 9 is the opening of canon's second arc (`foundation/TIMELINE.md` §5): *"six months later."* The Aroras are dead; the
girl the kul calls **Ananta** walks into the Suri house as **Ahana Dhawan**, a property magnate who owns the mine the
Naagmani sleeps under. Everything in the episode is the family trying to keep her close enough to rob.

## §1 · The beats

### Part one — the crocodiles' bill, and the stone (00:04–03:15)

- **B01 · C.** *"Kuch bhi karke humein Naagmani laani hogi, warna ye magarmach humein kachcha chaba jayenge."* The crocodile man
  has thrown a man who served him thirty years to the animals, to show the others what the bill looks like. A man begs.
- **B02 · C.** Aryaman is called urgently. *"Dad, Aryaman, mujhe tumse bahut zaroori baat karni hai. Jaldi aake milo."*
- **B03 · C.** Parmeet sets him on it: *"Net pe search kar, apne logon ko dauda… itna bada channel hai hamara."*
- **B04 · C.** The stone's power, said out loud in that house: *"Woh naagmani agar koi beemar chhu le to theek ho jata hai. Mara
  hua insaan bhi zinda ho jata hai."* Aryaman: *"Main aisi kahaniyon mein believe nahin karta."* Parmeet: *"Beta, ye kahani real
  hai."*

### Part two — the mine, and the friend (03:15–12:14)

- **B05 · C.** Roma brings the file: people claiming to have seen it, **the Attari border**, a photograph that will not upload.
- **B06 · C.** Aryaman and **Vikram**: the best friend, the broken-hearted man, the engagement nobody has told Vikram about.
  *"Yeh duniya mein wohi ek banda hai jise main as my brother-in-law…"*
- **B07 · C.** The engagement is **today**. The guests are in the house. Pinky is dressed for it. Vikram does not know.
- **B08 · C.** The old photograph of a stone brighter than the sun; the mine beyond Delhi, at a village near Attari; and the
  question that turns the episode: *"Ye factory ka owner kaun hai? Ye mine kahan hai?"* — *"Ahana Dhawan. Ye hai property ki
  maard."* An appointment is to be fixed.

### Part three — the announcement, and the refusal (12:14–17:44)

- **B09 · C.** **A brother comes back after months** — *"Bhai, Karan ki shaadi ho gayi aur tu gayab raha… main kuch mahine pehle
  aana chahta tha par life mein kuch cheezein…"* — and the house closes round the return as if it costs nothing. **`open`: the
  track does not name him.** `R18` discipline applies: the page does not borrow an impostor's real name for him.
- **B10 · C.** Aryaman takes the floor and announces it: **Pinky and Vikram's engagement, today.**
- **B11 · C.** Vikram refuses in front of the room: *"Ye main uske liye laaya hoon jisse main sachcha pyaar karta hoon, aur woh tum
  nahin ho. Main kisi aur se pyaar karta hoon."* Pinky is humiliated in her own hall. Karan goes for him.
- **B12 · C.** Vikram will not raise a hand back: *"Tujhe jitna maarna hai maar le. Main tujhko ulta haath nahin uthaunga. Pyaar
  pe kisi ka zor nahin chalta."* — and Radhika's name is used against Aryaman in his own hall.

### Part four — Ahana (17:44–26:53)

- **B13 · C.** The woman Vikram loves walks in: **Ahana Dhawan**. *"Ye wohi hai jisse main pyaar karta hoon. The love of my life."*
- **B14 · C.** The family's contempt, and her price: *"Lakhon mein nahin, crore ki baat karti hai. Chhoti rakam mein maanegi ye
  ladki."* She does not need the room: *"Baby, I don't think so. Welcome. I should better leave."*
- **B15 · C.** **Parmeet recognises the name** — the upload lands — *"Ye to Ahana Dhawan. Humein is ladki ko kisi bhi haalat mein
  rokna hoga"* — because she is the way to the mine.
- **B16 · C.** The apology, at speed, in front of everybody: *"Main gusse mein tumse kuch zyada hi bhool gaya… mere ghar mein bhi
  betiyan hain."* She is invited to stay **two or three days** as a guest. The engagement is *"turant."*
- **B17 · C.** The family, out of her hearing, counting: *"Bas ek baar ye ladki humare ghar mein rehne aa jaye, to bhai sahab,
  naagmani humari."* The guest room is prepared; the lehnga is discussed.

### Part five — the house at night (27:30–33:42)

- **B18 · C.** Ahana on love and betrayal: *"Wafa karne waale ke hisse hi dagabazi likhi jaati hai. Mujhe bhi mila tha ek aisa
  veer hai. Par iska matlab ye to nahin ki main dobara pyaar nahin kar sakti."*
- **B19 · C.** Pinky to Aryaman, plotting in her own house: *"Dad ne sirf paison ke liye us ladki ko ghar mein entry de di…"*
  — *"Mere bhai ho na, abhi shaadi nahin hone doge"* — *"I am warning you."*
- **B20 · C, and the episode's spine.** **Alone, the naagin says what she is**: *"To kaisa lag raha hai? Finally aa hi gayi is ghar
  mein. Chhupana mushkil ho raha hai na tere liye?"* — *"In logon ne mujhse mera sab kuch cheen liya. Mamma, Papa, Rohan, aur
  aap."* — *"Aryaman se pyaar Purvi ne kiya tha. Ahana Aryaman se sirf nafrat karti hai."*
  **In this serial both of them are said, with the name substituted, and that is the whole of the ruling.** Our words are: *"Vihaan se
  pyaar Purvi ne kiya tha. Ahana Vihaan se sirf nafrat karti hai."* The show's man is never in a sentence with her love anywhere in this
  book; our man is, in this sentence, in her own mouth, and every other word stands where the episode put it (`O044`, `BF-84`, `BF-86`).
  **Ruled three times inside two days, and the third is the rule (`foundation/RIPPLE.md` R-01; `OPEN.md` O044).** (1) The ruling of 2026-11-12 kept the words and
  labelled them the account she gives the house — a label is not a re-derivation. (2) His first word, 2026-11-13: *"there should never mention of Aryaman
  because of butterfly effects… Aryaman had nothing to do with anything in ours"* — which this workshop read as *delete the sentence*, and emptied the
  beat instead. (3) His word the same night, and it is the rule: *"Just a simple thing, you just need to replace vihaan instead of aryman due to everything, but you
  do but not this."*
  **So, for any chapter drawing this row: the show's beat entire, with the name changed.** The question in a dead sister's cadence and the answer in her own voice; the
  accusation and the denial in that order; the denial said twice, the second time louder than a guest room at midnight carries, thrown at a shut door, nobody
  answering; and his staging verbatim — *"She says it to the dark of the room, in the voice she uses downstairs."* The money-and-a-son cover this hour already
  gives her (B04—B13) stays, because it is why she can say any of it aloud. **The pair is Vihaan's** (`WRITING.md` §0.1 #1), and the woman who came to Purvi with
  the knife was **Brinda in Vihaan's face**, as Chapter 17's ninth key rules it: *"that was not Aryaman, it was she."*

### Part six — the snake, and the vow (33:42–37:45)

## §2 · The rulings, made before the prose

1. **Ahana is Purvi, and the page never explains it.** Canon says it in B20 with her own mouth, alone. The house does not
   recognise her — not Parmeet, who met her, not Aryaman, who worked with her, not Karan. **The page gives the fact and no
   mechanism** (`canon/SUPERNATURAL.md` §1 rule 1). Whatever the show would render, the page does not describe, and no character
   in that house says the word *naagin* over her in Ep 9.
2. **Vihaan does not know her, and his heart does** — and this is the shape the author ordered on 2026-11-12, replacing the way this
   page first wrote it. (`O034` still binds: no recognition *by face or voice*, no speech in which he works out what she is, no grief
   the narration calls love. `SUPERNATURAL.md` §2 rule 5 and `O033` still bind: the pull is not knowledge and it is never named.)
   What changes is **how much of it is shown and whether he shuts up about it**: at the first glance the frame obeys her, the lamp dips,
   the back of his neck knows her before his face does; and when **Vikram takes her hand** he is jealous, and he does not know why, and
   he explains it wrongly — the heat, the crowd, the fact that his friend has combed his hair with water. **He talks while all of this is
   happening.** He ran a floor for six months before he got on a plane; the house's errands, the chairs, the trunk, the room number, a
   joke answered with a joke. The old line here read *"and says nothing"*, and the author's words on it were:
   *"Vihaan should talk or others things, you make him silent, that is completely wrong."* **The page never lets him know**, and it does
   not get to make him mute to achieve that.
3. **The thirteen faces stay canon's** (`CANON.md` R16). Nothing in Ep 9 reopens the pit.
4. **The Naagmani's power is only ever a claim in a mouth.** *"Mara hua insaan bhi zinda ho jata hai"* is Parmeet's sentence, in
   his house, to his son. The page does not test it, does not confirm it, and does not let the narration agree.
5. **The mine is the plot's machinery and stays property.** Papers, a price per acre, an appointment, a woman who will not take a
   small number. No glow, no pull, no map with a cross on it.
6. **The returning brother (B09) is canon's and is not named on the page.** This adaptation has its own man coming back after
   months (`O034`), and he must not be written into B09's mouth as if the track had said his name. **How the two returns sit
   together is the chapter's first decision and is recorded in its footer.**
7. **Parmeet's cruelty to the snake (B22) is the episode's second spine and is not softened.** It is the one act in the episode
   that shows what that house is when there is no audience, and B23 shows it failing.
8. **Pinky's humiliation is real and is not comic.** She is refused in her own hall by the man she was dressed for, and then she
   goes to her brother and asks for a marriage to be stopped. The page lets both be true.
9. **The vow is the episode's last line and is written as a vow**, in her voice, at full scale, with nobody there to hear it
   (`STYLE_CANON.md` §1d).
10. **No new power appears in Ep 9.** The naag material of the episode is: a name, a memory, a hatred, a been that does not
    work, and a threat delivered to a house. `canon/SUPERNATURAL.md` rule 3 — every one of them costs somebody by the end.
11. **Canon contradicts itself about what Vikram knows, and the page keeps the contradiction.** Ep 6, 27:25, has Dimple say it
    in the front room — *"mere Vikram aur Pinky ki shaadi mein yeh sab nahi chalega"* — and that sentence is written, faithfully,
    in **Chapter 15**. Ep 9 then runs the engagement as a surprise nobody has told the man about: *"but usey engagement ke baare
    mein toh pata hi nahin"* (07:28), *"humne Vikram ko bataya bhi nahin"* (07:55). **Both are canon.** The page does not pick
    one, does not smooth it, and does not have anybody in the hall explain it: a house that plans a wedding out loud and never
    tells the groom is exactly the house this is, and Ep 9's hall is where that habit is paid for.
12. **Vikram is Rishabh's son and Aryaman's childhood friend** — not a Suri, and not a cousin (`foundation/CHARACTERS.md`). No
    cousin in this serial carries his name, so that the reader never has to decide which Vikram a room means (*Chapter 14*,
    corrected 2026-11-12).

## §3 · Where Vihaan can stand, and where he cannot

He has been gone six months (`O034`); the episode is his return, and the return is the one thing canon does not have.

| Beat | May he be in it? | How |
|---|---|---|
| B01–B04 | **No.** The sheds are closed to him and always have been | — |
| B05 | Yes, at the channel, on the other side of the file | He is the man who would ask who owns the mine; Roma is the one who answers |
| B06–B08 | Yes, at the edge of the engagement | A brother back after months, in a house dressed for a party that is about to be destroyed |
| B09 | **No, not into canon's mouth.** His return is his own scene | The chapter decides how the two returns sit (`§2` ruling 6) |
| B10–B12 | Yes | He is in the hall when Vikram refuses. He does not fight anyone: he puts himself between Karan and the two men holding him, tells Karan what a broken thing costs in a house with a wedding in it, and straightens his brother's tie — and then he counts the chairs out loud, because that is what he has to say that the room can hear |
| B13–B16 | Yes, and this is the danger | Ahana walks in. He does not *know* her — his heart does, at the first glance, and the jealousy arrives with Vikram's hand on hers, and neither is ever named. **Played at full length** (the author's order of 2026-11-12 struck the old cap of "two paragraphs at most"), and he has lines in the scene: the room number, the chairs, the trunk, the one sentence to Vikram that is too sharp to be only a joke |
| B17 | Yes | The guest room, the lehnga, the arrangements — paper work, which is his |
| B18–B20 | **No.** B20 is hers alone | — |
| B21–B23 | **No.** The snake and the been are closed rooms | — |
| B24 | **No.** The vow is hers, with nobody there | — |

**The shape this gives the chapter:** he is in the house, in the return, in the hall, in the guest room's arrangements, and out
of every room where the naag world is actually spoken. That is `canon/SUPERNATURAL.md` §2 in practice, and it is also the only
honest way to keep canon's episode intact.
