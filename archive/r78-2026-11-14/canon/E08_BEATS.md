## §2 · The rulings, made before the prose

1. **The girl who says "Ananta" is the girl the house called Purvi.** `CANON.md` R5, and B10 is canon's own confirmation. The page does
   not explain the doctrine; it lets her say the name and lets the men laugh at it, and the kul in the hall understand.
2. **The thirteen faces are canon's, and the page does not name any of them.** The men count what they can see and stop counting.
3. **The grave is the men's, not the kul's.** B01's two voices are split: the men at the pit, the kul over the temple. No one in that
   party hears the other.
4. **The crocodile man is not named.** The track gives him no name. He is the five men's handler and their own country's voice, and he
   is written as a man who feeds animals and cancels leave.
5. **The PSA is the house's own advertisement.** Canon has the family sell the story of its own ruined wedding as a public warning. The
   page keeps the exact irony and does not comment on it.
6. **Whose hand sent the hacked message is `open` in canon and stays `open` here.** The page gives the men the question and gives them
   no answer.
7. **Aryaman's Manohar is canon and stays cruel.** He is not softened, and nothing is added to explain him.
8. **The dragon is not shown.** Same ruling as Episode 7: the animal is a paragraph.
