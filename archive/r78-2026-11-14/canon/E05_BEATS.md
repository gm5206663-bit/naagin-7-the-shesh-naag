## 2 · The rulings made before the prose

1. **B13's speaker is Vihaan.** The track gives Parmeet a question about the Kumbh, Pasha, three phone calls and readiness, and gives the answering voice no name. In this serial the family's London end and the Kumbh pitch are his (`foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` §3a), so the answering voice is the son who runs it, and the beat is his one scene with his father in the episode. It is a *ruling*, not a fact, and it is reversible.
2. **B33's "the man the whole world wants" is Vihaan, and the page never names him.** Canon's referent is Aryaman, because in canon Aryaman is the man she loves in this arc. In this serial she comes to love Vihaan (`O016`), and the line's own wording — *jise poori duniya pasand karti hai* — fits him exactly as `O025` frames him: the room looks at him. So the line stays, the name stays out, and nothing on the page says whose face is in her head.
3. **B23–B24 are Aryaman's and stay Aryaman's.** The car, the eyelash and his contempt for love are canon's, and they are the clearest statement the season makes of who he is. What he is *not* given: any touch, any display, any move towards her. The scene is a boss driving his assistant to work and being rude about love.
4. **B28 is an omen and the room makes a joke of it.** *Kechuli wali* — the office laughs at a girl whose skin is peeling. That is `§1c` done the way the show does it and it is not softened.
5. **B14–B16 name nobody.** The track calls the man *Samir* and leaves the woman's name to a sari; the husband is unnamed. The page uses *Samir*, keeps the wife off the page entirely, and gives the husband no name he is not given.
6. **B27's locked-door beat is the wrong face.** A woman says she was locked in the washroom while somebody was in the office wearing her; the room decides she is joking. It is canon's own grain, it is the preparation `SITUATIONS.md` §11 requires, and the page does not underline it.
7. **The Mahasapera is a woman** (*Maa Sapela ji*), and she arrives at the end of the episode, at the wedding house, and is not described beyond the door.
8. **Nothing about the animal's name** (`S-20`), nothing about what Purvi is becoming beyond what the bites did (`S-16` territory), nothing read from her own state that canon has not said.
