## The king's four lines (`R21`)

Episode 1's litany, as this workshop now writes it, names the king who comes with her — four short lines, with the name after them taken by a blow (`R21`, `O023`).

**This episode is where that must pay or cost nothing.** The litany is quoted twice in Episode 2: once by Aryaman's sister, reading it off her memory of the story, and once by the men in the room recalling what the scientist said. **Both times canon gives only the queen's half, and stops.**

**Ruling:** the king's lines **never entered the rumour.** The men who beat the prophet in 2001 heard four sentences they did not understand, and the version they carried out of that room is the version that reaches this house in 2025: neither woman nor man, ichchhadhari naagin, Shesh naagin. The king was said once, in a dark room, to men who wanted only one answer, and the word after it never came out of the prophet's mouth. **So nothing in the enemy's hands needs changing, and `OPEN-17` holds untouched: nobody in this house is looking for a king, because nobody in this house ever heard of one.**
- What the reader is owed: the thin pleasure of hearing a house full of killers recite a prophecy that has a hole in it they cannot see.
