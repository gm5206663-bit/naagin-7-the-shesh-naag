  bracket is not mistaken for a gap in the file: the track prints the syllable **inline** in the 28:36 block —
  *"…पहले नहीं कापा।"* — with no bracket and no speaker. The bracket is this ledger's hedge that कापा is a mis-hearing;
  the chapter's silence is the honest treatment of a word the ASR is not sure of.
- **And *sampark* is a restoration, not a transcription** (written into this row on 2026-11-14, because Chapter 12 had been citing the row as if the row had heard it). The ASR's *samaj* is almost certainly a mis-hearing of a word in the music; the workshop reads it as **sampark** (*contact, connection*) because that is the device the episode is using — the watchers ask for a way to reach her, and a lamp leans toward a hand — and the chapter titles its scene on the reading. Nothing else is built on it: the word is spoken twice and explains nothing.
