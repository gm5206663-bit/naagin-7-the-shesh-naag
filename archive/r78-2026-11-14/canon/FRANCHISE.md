# The franchise before this season

**Owns: what the seasons before this one give us — the Naagmani's rules, rebirth, the ranks of the naag world — and the short list of things never taken from them.** It is franchise ground (**F**), not Season 7 canon (**C**); where the two differ, Season 7 wins. Read with `CANON.md`, which holds this season's own witnesses.

The author asked me to read the seasons before this one for understanding. This page is what they give us, kept separately from `CANON.md`, because these are not facts of Season 7 — they are the ground the whole franchise stands on. Where Season 7 has spoken for itself, Season 7 wins. Where it has not, the older seasons are the best evidence of what a naag story in this world can do.

Tag: **F** = franchise ground, from the earlier seasons. **C** = this season's own canon. A thing marked F is not automatically true in Season 7; it is legal, and it is likely.

## The Naagmani and who may touch it

| What the older seasons show | Tag | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The Naagmani is the gem with power that every season's killers want, and the reason naagins die. | F | S1 plot, all seasons |
| **Not everyone can touch it.** In S1 only Ritik and his father could, because of a mark on their bodies (Suryavanshi). Later seasons widen it: the Naagrani of the Shesh Naag clan, and those born with Ashlesha Nakshatra. | F | S1; S2; franchise discussion |
| **Ritik, S1, is "the protector of the Naagmani"** — a man, married to the naagin, whose whole function in the story is to keep the pearl. | F | S1 cast and plot |
| The pearl can take power away: in S2 a naagin's powers are gone through it; in S6 a naagin uses it to erase another's memory. | F | S2; S6 |
| The Shesh Naagin line is made by the pearl's power: in S6, a family of the Vasuki clan becomes Shesh Naagin by absorbing it, and a Shesh Naagin can be made only from the five vansh (Shesh, Takshak, Vasuki and two more). | F | S6 |
| S6 multiplies it to eight manis, sealed on a statue. | F | S6 |

**What this means for us.** "The Naagmani knows him very well" (`O022`) is not a new kind of statement in this franchise: the pearl has always had opinions about who may hold it, and it has always had a doorkeeper who is a man. Season 7 has said nothing yet about how its Naagmani is touched. If the pearl is contested in this season the way Ep 38 onwards suggests, the rule of who may lift it is ours to state once, and it must be stated before it is used.

## Rebirth

| What the older seasons show | Tag | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Death and return is the franchise's spine: a naagin killed comes back for vengeance; S2 ends with the lead praying to Shiva for justice and the payoff arriving in S3. | F | S1–S3 |
| S6 runs a full rebirth: Pragati and Raghav are Prathna and Raghu born again, and a character asks that the enemy not learn they took rebirth. | F | S6 |

**What this means for us.** `O021` is not an import; it is the franchise's normal way of continuing a life. Season 7 uses it differently — the rebirth is a *naag*, not a naagin, and the memory never returns (`O022`) — which is the one place this season's version is sharper than the old ones.

## The ranks

| What the older seasons show | Tag | Where |
|---|---|---|
| S3 is the season of Naagrani and Mahanaagrani politics, with a clan's claim on the pearl. | F | S3 |
| S4 brings a Naagrani's daughter, a princess. | F | S4 |
| S5 brings the Maha Adinaagin, the first and most powerful naagin. | F | S5 |
| S6 has a Shesh Naagin marry the **Sarvashreshth Shesh Naag**, and has Takshak grant a Shesh Naagin one wish. | F | S6 |
| Season 7's own words: the highest of the hundred-year ichchhadhari naag-naagins are **Mahashesh Naagraj** and **Mahanagrani**; the strongest of all is the naagin who is *born* to it. | C | Ep 5 00:24:14 |

**What this means for us.** Every season outranks the last. Season 7 has already said the born naagin is above the hundred-year ones, and has named the male rank Mahashesh Naagraj. The author's `O021` — Vihaan as a Sheshnaag holding that rank in his last life — sits inside the ladder rather than beside it. The ladder is why his power has to be bounded (`OPEN-15`): the franchise's habit of escalating is exactly what would eat this story.

## What is *not* taken from the older seasons

- Names: no Shivanya, Ritik, Shesha, Shivangi, Rocky, Bela, Ruhi, Pratha, Pragati (as a person of this season's past), Mehek, Takshak-as-a-character.
- Events: no season-1 revenge, no Mahishmati, no bee-queen, no eight manis, no statue.
- Ritik is not Vihaan. Both are men who keep a pearl; that is where it stops. Vihaan is a naag, has no memories, and is not marked by a clan. (`O012` still holds: nothing crosses in from the author's other projects either.)
