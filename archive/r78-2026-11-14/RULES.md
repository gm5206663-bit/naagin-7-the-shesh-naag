# RULES — the gate and the pass

**Owns: what "done" means for a chapter of this project.**
These are the author's existing rules, gathered from the author's own files and applied to this show. This file adds no rule of its own. Where a line comes from is given in brackets.

---

## 1 · The pass, one canon episode at a time

```
1. LOAD      read STATUS.md, OPEN.md, foundation/WORLD_STATE.md, the OC file, the previous chapter's footer
2. VERIFY    read the episode's whole caption track and every recap of it; note what neither can show
3. MAP       write the episode's beat ledger in source order (canon/E##_BEATS.md)
4. PLAN      canon scenes are the spine; mark where the OC's thread runs beside them and where it touches
5. WRITE     chapters/
6. AUDIT     re-read as an enemy editor against the gate below; repair before delivery
7. RECORD    every file the chapter moved, in the same pass (list in foundation/FOUNDATION.md, "After every chapter")
```
[StoryOS pipeline, Framework v4.0; The Twin `RULES.md` §2; `storyos-master-skill.md` §6]

One canon episode is one unit of work. An episode is written across several chapters, each made of full scenes. [`storyos-master-skill.md` §5: "3-4+ chapters per episode; full scenes"]

A chapter may not teach canon. If the prose needs a fact no ledger row holds, go back to the source. [The Twin `RULES.md` §2]

## 2 · The gate

| # | Gate | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Episode reference** | The chapter does not open by naming the episode and part it covers |
| 2 | **Canon** | A canon detail has no row in a beat ledger, or contradicts its row |
| 3 | **Order** | Canon beats are reordered, merged or skipped without the footer saying so |
| 4 | **Spine** | The canon scene is not the bulk of the chapter, or the OC becomes its centre |
| 5 | **Ownership** | The OC takes a canon character's action, line or victory; or a canon beat goes differently with no cause on the page and no line under CHANGED in the footer |
| 6 | **Information** | Anyone knows, says or acts on something they have no path to knowing at that point in the broadcast order |
| 7 | **Names** | A name is used before the show has said it, or a name is invented for a canon person |
| 8 | **Staging** | Authored action, looks or dialogue are presented as if canon, without the footer declaring them |
| 9 | **Mechanism** | A rule of how naags, the Naagmani, the dragon or any power works is not taken from the show |
| 10 | **Talent** | The Adaptation Talent speaks, chooses, counts or shows a menu; or anything supernatural appears in him before the awakening (`foundation/ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` §7) |
| 11 | **Scenes** | A scene is summarised instead of played; a chapter opens without place, time, who is present and what they were doing |
| 12 | **Voice** | A character speaks out of their register: the Punjabi of the Arora and Suri elders, Aryaman's clipped half-English sarcasm, the naagins' formal Hindi |
| 13 | **Ending** | The chapter ends on a vague promise instead of a concrete image or line |
| 14 | **Coverage** | A beat of the stretch is neither written, nor marked already written, changed or deferred (a beat may be changed, and a new beat may be added, provided `STORY.md` §1 laws 1 and 3 hold) (`WRITING.md` §4) |
| 15 | **Romance** | It moves by coincidence or by the narrator's say-so; or takes a step that cannot be taken back without the author's word (`foundation/RELATIONSHIPS.md` §4) |
| 16 | **Camera** | The past tense in narration, or a sentence past sixty words (`WRITING.md` §2; `tools/camera_check.py`). **A comparison in narration fails nothing** (`O037`: *like*, *as if*, *as though* are ordinary narration now — the author was asked and cut the ban entirely), and **a named feeling no longer fails anything** — the author deleted that rule on 2026-11-12 (`O035`), and no gate, tool or file may restore it. |
| 17 | **Him** | He is in a scene and nothing is different because he is there; or he is made foolish, or made less than he is, so that a scene can happen (`WRITING.md` §4; `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md`) |
| 18 | **Episode density** | A scene the episode plays is summarised, a thing the episode shows is left out, or the room, the hour or the weather is not on the page; or a sound, a person or an event is added that the episodes do not have (`WRITING.md` §2 law 6) |
| 19 | **He is in the chapter** | A chapter with Vihaan in fewer than two scenes, or a chapter whose footer does not declare where he is on its `OC:` line — a scene may leave him out, a chapter may not (`foundation/OC_PRESENCE.md`; `tools/oc_check.py`) | A chapter of mine, 2026-11-11: faithful, legal, and not this serial |
| 20 | **He is seen** | No line on the page showing what he looks like, or a line that disclaims it (*that is all it does*, *it wins him nobody's trust*). At least two places per chapter where somebody looks, and always shown, never explained, never praised by the narration (`O025`; `WRITING.md` §5a; `tools/oc_check.py` OC6 and OC7) | Five chapters in which not one person looked at him; the author: *"you completely ignoreing how handsome he is, like you know what is he"* |
| 21 | **An absence has a life in it** | A year of his treated as a blank; a stretch away with nothing concrete in it; **a stretch away built out of another genre's material** (a foreign career, a foreign cast, a foreign city with its own weather); **or a stretch away that shrinks him** — a boy fetching papers, *small work*, a post with no standing. Three tests, all of them: no blank; in this show's terms; and at his own size (`O026`; `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` §3a) | Three attempts in one day: the blank, the British news drama, and *"small London work"* — *"you serious Nerfing oc why?"* |
| 22 | **His standing** | He is written as the help, the errand boy, the pretty one who is harmless — or a chapter passes with nothing showing what he can do (a chapter in which he is an infant is the one exception, and it says so). He may carry a tray (the rooms are closed to him) but the **narration never treats him as a servant**, and every chapter has at least one beat where his capacity or his authority shows and lands (`WRITING.md` §5b; `tools/oc_check.py` OC8) | The same instinct as the missing face: fear of making him too much, applied to his work instead of his looks |
| 23 | **Nothing is improvised** | A person, room, habit, debt, object or piece of the past used in a chapter without existing in the foundations — or a chapter written without first asking what it will need and designing what is missing. The workshop designs ahead of the page, not only after the author names a gap (`WRITING.md` §4 item 8; `foundation/HOUSE_HISTORY.md`) | *"you can't just fix things only do them there is could you needed to degine everything when needed"* |
| 24 | **A meeting is a scene** | Two people who matter to each other meet, and the page gives them a hand-off of courtesies: no friction, no physical beat, no repeated line, no object changing hands, no held look, no interruption, no omen. This show stages a meeting (`CANON.md` R26; `WRITING.md` §1a) | Vihaan and Purvi's first meeting was written as *"congratulations"* / *"thank you"* on a step — the author: *"what the joke you make of Vihaan and Purvi Frist meeting like canon style completely died"* |
| 25 | **The picture** | A scene opens on a face instead of a place; the coded light is absent; no object gets its own frame; more than 65% of a scene's paragraphs are bare speech — the author, 2026-11-12: *"you don't write the seen, the picture and visual and others correctly, according to canon style."* Canon spends its first two and a half minutes of Episode 1 on place and colour before one line is spoken (`canon/PICTURE.md`; `tools/picture_check.py`) | Eighteen chapters written like a radio play: speech in 54% to 69% of paragraphs, and not one insert shot in most scenes |
| 25 | **Canon is played at its own length** | A canon scene written shorter than the episode plays it — the violence in four lines, a prophecy at half its speech, a throne that is a cloth over a chair. Density is not a word count; it is whether the page lasts as long as the scene does. `canon/STYLE_AUDIT.md` is the standing check | Chapter 4's thinnest scenes were all canon's own, not the OC's: the lynching at four lines, the parapet at half length, the throne as furniture |
| 26 | **Butterflies registered** | A `CHANGED:` or `ADDED:` in a chapter with no numbered entry in `foundation/BUTTERFLY.md`; an entry with no cost written; a renumbering; or a butterfly quietly deleted instead of marked `WITHDRAWN` — **one line the day it is made, never renumbered, the cost always written** (`O036`; `STYLE_SHEET.md` §4; `foundation/BUTTERFLY.md`) | The register was built from a footer dump that had been cut off mid-book, and twenty-two butterflies were missing; the author's order is *"don't forget even single butterfly effect"* |
| 27 | **The score keeps its loyalties** | A romantic cue attaches to Aryaman and Purvi; two registers play at once; a melody is written for Vihaan, who is allowed one low string and no tune (`canon/SOUND_AND_SCREEN.md` §9; `tools/score_check.py`, the author's ruling of 2026-11-12) |
| 28 | **The frame at both ends, and the promise is only of what is there** | A chapter head without its twelve seconds of theme, or a chapter foot (1–18) without a precap, or a precap whose threat line or image cannot be found verbatim in the chapter it points at, or any frame block written as a `## ` heading so it enters the ledger as a scene (`canon/TITLES_SEQUENCE.md` §III–§IV; `tools/build_frame.py`, the author's order of 2026-11-12) |
| 29 | **He is not written silent** | A chapter puts him in three or more scenes and gives him fewer than two lines, or a scene hands him a silence phrase (*says nothing*, *does not answer*, *not a word*) with no reason on the footer's `SILENT:` line (`WRITING.md` §0.1, `O040`; `tools/oc_check.py` OC9–OC10) |
| 31 | **A change that is still live has a due date** | A row in `foundation/FORWARD.md` carries a status or a magnitude the vocabulary does not allow; or its `last paid` names a chapter whose footer does not cite it; or an `ACTIVE` row whose horizon names chapters has gone past them with no chapter paying it; or a number is cited that the register does not hold (`foundation/RIPPLE.md` §2 items 7 and 8; `tools/divergence_clock.py`, FC1 to FC4, wired here) |
| 30 | **Canon carries its ripple with it** | A chapter's prose asserts a fact a registered divergence has superseded without owning the rule on the footer's `RIPPLED:` line; or the show's words are used verbatim **and** carry such a fact with no verdict on the footer's `PASTED:` line; or the chapter carries no `RIPPLED:` declaration at all. `tools/ripple_check.py` RP1 to RP5, against `foundation/RIPPLE.md` §3b. *Why: the author, 2026-11-12 — core problem is that you paste canon. Nineteen chapters had their set-pieces rewritten and their sentences copied.* |

**Hard fails, no repair in the same pass:** a fact from memory; a recap treated as the episode; two sources averaged; an invented mechanism; a canon outcome changed for convenience; a character acting on what they do not know.

## 4 · What is the author's alone

On 2026-11-11 the author gave leave to proceed on the workshop's own judgement once the foundation and Sara stood and the canon was understood (`O009`). That covers building, reading, planning and drafting. It does not cover what `OPEN.md` §2 lists: his age and birth, the reason he is gone on the Episode 7 night, whom she loves, Aryaman's place, the awakening and its form, any death or reveal that canon does not have. Those are laid before him with the evidence, at the point where they begin to block. A draft is never called accepted until he accepts it. His work is public by his own standing preference, so dated snapshots of this workshop on Drive are shared by link; other people's material (recap texts, stills, caption tracks) is not. Nothing is pushed to GitHub without his word.
