## 1 · What "in sync" was taken to mean

Six tests, run over every file in the workshop:

1. **Numbers agree.** Every live word count, beat count, file count and dated-entry count in a record matches the file it describes.
2. **Paths resolve.** Every `` `path` `` in every `.md` either exists here, or is declared to live elsewhere.
3. **Live statements are true today.** A sentence that says *this is the state* is true now; a sentence that says *this is what happened* stays as it was written.
4. **Footers are whole.** All seven chapters carry `OC:` `BASE:` `EVIDENCE:` `WITNESS:` `STAGING:` `SEEN:` `CHANGED:` `DEFERRED:`.
5. **Tables are load-bearing.** Gate table, coverage tables, star maps, scene ladders: no row out of order, no total that does not add up.
6. **Nothing is owed and unmarked.** A checkpoint promised after every chapter exists, or the gap is named.

- **`Anant Kul`, `Poornima`, `Ichchhadhari`/`Ichhadhari`** — spelling variation that lives in the recaps and the captions respectively; not levelled, because the witness decides the spelling.
- **Historical entries in `HISTORY.md`, `CHECK_2026-11-11.md`, `Sara/SARA.md` and `Sara/docs/03_the_growth_ledger.md`** that say *six chapters* or *26,030 words*. They were true when written and are not rewritten. Only live statements were updated.
