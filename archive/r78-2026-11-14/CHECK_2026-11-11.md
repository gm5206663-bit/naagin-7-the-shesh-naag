## 2 · What was wrong, and what was done

**1 · Purvi's age — the one real continuity fault.** Chapter 4 had her say *"Twenty-four."* and then *"That is next year."* Both cannot be true. `foundation/TIMELINE.md` and canon have her **twenty-three** in this stretch, with the twenty-fourth birthday the thing canon keeps naming. The line now reads *"I am twenty-three. It is not my birthday yet."* and `canon/E02_BEATS.md`'s age row carries the birthday-not-an-age wording, so the next chapter cannot make the same mistake.

**2 · A scene heading in the wrong spelling.** Chapter 4's prophecy scene was titled `## The Naagrani of the Anant Kul`. The prophecy's line in this world is *Naagrani of the Anant kul* — and headings are copy. Corrected in the chapter, in `canon/STYLE_AUDIT.md`, in `STATUS.md` and in `HISTORY.md`.

**3 · Chapter 6's camera direction contradicted itself.** The corridor paragraph had Vihaan *taking the camera up to the shelf on five*; the closing lines then had him wait for the lift to come back up and ride it **down**. A man cannot be carrying the kit up and down at once. The cause is now on the page and points one way: the bay has sent for a lens and he is the one carrying it **down**, the lift has taken his father down to the shutter with him waiting, and he rides it down after. The footer note and `foundation/OC_PRESENCE.md`'s row were corrected to match — a fix that leaves the map describing the old text is not a fix.

**4 · One sentence closed two chapters.** Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 both ended on *"He stays on the roof a while longer."* A repeated exit line is a tic, not a motif: it flattens both. Chapter 4's close is now its own image — the dust in the air, the windows going dark under him — and Chapter 3 keeps the original.

**5 · `Poornima` in `STORY.md`, `Purnima` in the prose.** The chapters use one spelling and the story book listed the other. The prose column now reads **Purnima**. The recap-facing files (`canon/SEASON.md`, `OPEN.md`, `foundation/ADAPTATION_MODULE.md`) keep *Poornima*, which is how the recaps spell it and how the workshop's canon record quotes them — a name is fixed once per register, and §4 of `STORY.md` says which register this page speaks in. Not a defect; declared here so it is not found again as one.

**6 · Chapter 2: he was in the house and in nothing.** `oc_check` passed — his name is in two places — but one of them is a voice through a stairwell at the close. That is a presence with no cause the reader can see and no consequence. The law is not "his name appears"; it is *a cause and a consequence*. A new scene, `## The Room on the Fourth Floor` (525 words), was written into the chapter where the chapter already sends him: up with his bag, into the room the house has kept ready, the jug with the glass over its mouth, the boy's shelf, Nisha on the arm of the chair with the pakoras, and **his own camera turned on him the wrong way round** — four seconds of film made of a brother by the one person in that house who records things and whom nobody has noticed recording them. The bag is never unpacked, and that is the only thing in that house that says he is not staying. It touches no canon beat, moves no canon line, and meets nobody: the first meeting is still the lens on Monday.

**7 · The audit's own tables had drifted.** `canon/STYLE_AUDIT.md` listed scene lengths that no longer matched the files — most off by a word or two, a handful by more than ten —, and one note described a scene that is not on the page — `The Inhaler`, whose note still spoke of an attack in the crowd that the scene no longer contains. Every count is now read out of the chapters, the note is rewritten to what the scene is, and the totals are recomputed: **26,030 words in the scenes**, chapter by chapter 4,741 · 3,851 · 3,024 · 5,129 · 3,305 · 5,980.
