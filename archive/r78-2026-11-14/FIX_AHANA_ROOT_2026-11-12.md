# THE AHANA ROOT — canon pasted without its ripple, found and fixed (2026-11-12)

**Owns: the dated record of one fault — the diagnosis in the author's words, what was changed, and what is now machine-enforced. The standing registry is `foundation/RIPPLE.md`; the pass is accounted in `STATUS.md` §87. This file keeps the evidence of what was checked, and the withdrawal of an offer that should never have been made.**

## 1 · What he stopped on

> "Aryaman se pyaar Purvi ne kiya tha." She says it to the dark of the room. "Ahana Aryaman se sirf nafrat karti hai."

`Chapter 19 · Chhupana Mushkil Ho Raha Hai`. Canon's own sentence, verbatim from Episode 9 (`canon/E09_BEATS.md` B20 — *"alone, the naagin says what she is"*, the episode's spine). Nothing in the transcription was wrong. He put six question marks on it and said: solve it **from the roots**. When this file first went up it offered three readings of the sentence as an open question; he answered the offer itself, and his answer is why the file now reads like this:

> *"it's your mistake pasting canon, you need to add butterfly effects and others All things, that you don't do, core problem is that you paste canon, you completely miss one of the most important part of my fen fiction."*

The three options were withdrawn on the spot. `OPEN.md` **O016** — **"Purvi loves Vihaan."**, confirmed by *"Yes, i meant it"* — has been in the workshop since the day before. The pair was never open; a pasted sentence had been allowed to override a standing ruling.

## 2 · The root, stated exactly

This book's divergences were applied to **set-pieces** and never to **the sentences between them**. The ledger moved the love to Vihaan again and again — `BF-13` the dance, `BF-15` the forged message on the terrace, `BF-76` the score struck out, and the theme, the ring, the first glance of `BF-81` — while no rule, gate, or habit required a borrowed canon line to be checked against any of them. So Chapter 19 copied B20 whole and every gate stayed green: **nothing counted which pasted sentence carries a fact this serial has already changed.**

Measured the same day, all nineteen chapters, every quotation of 22 or more characters matched against the show's own files:

- **5** verbatim show lines in prose outside Chapter 19; **40** inside it. Using the show's Hindi is the workshop's method, not a fault.
- The subset that contradicts a registered divergence: **Chapter 19's spine line** (against `O016`) and — found only once the registry's first rules existed and were run — **Chapter 10's sideboard beat**, ending *"…and he takes his glass away and says nothing to anybody"*: the same muzzle clause struck out of Chapter 19 hours earlier, still green on every check the workshop owns.

## 3 · What the pages now say

**Chapter 19** — the show's two lines stay in her mouth, and the scene shows them being *used*:

> That is the account she gives this house, and most of it happened. A girl carries files to a desk on the fourth floor and a lift laughs at her. … Say a thing often enough and the walls take it over.
>
> One name is missing from that account, and she has left it out by hand, the way she shuts the trunk.

**Chapter 10** — the silence becomes a question, and the gossip goes on as if it had not been heard:

> "Kaunsa zone?" he says into the ice bucket, and his voice carries the length of the hall and no further. "Aur kiske liye. Batana toh padega."

**SUPERSEDED the next day, in part.** The Chapter 10 repair stands. The Chapter 19 one does not: keeping the sentence and labelling it an account left the man in her history, which is what his ruling of 2026-11-13 forbids (`OPEN.md` O044, `BF-84`). The scene is re-written without him, not re-tagged.

No canon line was moved to another mouth and none was cut. `canon/E09_BEATS.md` B20 now carries the ruling **inside the beat file**, so the next chapter drawn from that row reads the verdict together with the line.

## 4 · The machine, so this does not depend on memory

`foundation/RIPPLE.md` is the registry: **R-01** whose her love is, **R-02** the pull played and never explained, **R-03** nobody writes him out of his own scenes, **R-04** no romantic cue between Aryaman and Purvi — each with a `superseded:` and an `allow:` regex that `tools/ripple_check.py` reads literally.

`tools/ripple_check.py` fails a chapter on **RP1** a superseded fact asserted in the scenes with the rule unowned on the footer's `RIPPLED:` line; **RP2** the show's words used verbatim *and* carrying a superseded fact, with no verdict on the footer's `PASTED:` line; **RP3** an invented rule id; **RP4** a registry regex that does not compile; **RP5** no declaration at all, so a chapter may not be clean merely because nobody looked. It runs inside `tools/check.py`: a paste of this kind now stops the workshop's own preflight.

Verified rather than asserted: the selftest is green on seven fixtures, and three regressions were planted in throwaway copies and each caught before being reverted — the `PASTED:` verdict deleted (RP2), the paste rewritten as narration's truth (RP2), the Chapter 10 muzzle clause restored (RP1 on R-03).

## 4b · The same look, a day later, at the prose (2026-11-13)

He read the re-derived scene and answered: *"This is not good either, this acutely give me hints of
your weakness and problems. Chopta short short sentence and choppy."* Two separate complaints, and the
first was mine to own: the repair had left **narration explaining the lie**. The paragraphs were these —

> That is the account she gives this house, and most of it happened.
> She has decided, twice now, that she is allowed to say it here. …
> Say a thing often enough and the walls take it over. … In her own mouth, to herself, it has gone
> soft around the edges, and that is the part she would not have predicted.

All of it summarised what the scene had already shown, which is the opposite of what the serial does:
it plays a woman's history in images and lets the audience count the cost. Struck. What stands now is
her saying the two lines, hearing herself, saying them again softer, and not getting the third ready;
and outside the room, Pinky at the panel of the small door and Parmeet deciding that repetition is
understanding — which is the public consequence the house law requires of any private fact.

The rhythm complaint was measured instead of argued. `camera_check.py` prints two figures on every
chapter now: **N8**, the share of narration under nine words (the book runs 19% to 47%; Chapter 19 was
at 42%, now 19%) and over forty-five; **N7**, each sentence where the voice explains a character's lie,
labels what a character "will remember", or addresses the reader with an aphorism. Both are printed, not
fatal: N7 flags six sentences in nineteen chapters and three of them read like this serial. They are his
to rule on, one at a time, as the style pass reaches each chapter. `WRITING.md` §9b, `L-32`, HISTORY.
