# BUTTERFLY — every butterfly effect in the book, in one register

**Owns: the complete list of everything this adaptation does that canon does not do.** One entry per butterfly, numbered, with where it was made and what it costs further on. **Nothing enters the story without a line here.** Where a chapter's footer names a change and this file does not, this file is wrong and the footer wins — add the line, do not write over it.

**The law (`STORY.md` §1, `RULES.md` L-24, `O017`).** The canon is the floor and not the ceiling. A butterfly is allowed only when it follows from the story's own people and their own acts; it may never contradict a canon beat, move a canon line to another mouth, or spend a canon reveal early. Every one of them is declared in the chapter's `CHANGED:` or `ADDED:` key on the day it is made.

**The one butterfly everything else hangs on:** `BF-01` — the child in the cot in 2001. He is the reason 2025 has a man in it who is not in canon at all.

**Size of the register:** **100 numbered entries, `BF-01` to `BF-100`, 99 of them live** (the block is not consecutive in the file: `BF-89` to `BF-91` were minted later and sit with their own chapters), of which `BF-82` is withdrawn with its number kept, and **all nineteen chapters have at least one.** `BF-01`–`BF-50` are the first sweep (2026-11-12, from a footer dump that was cut off mid-book); `BF-51`–`BF-72` are the second sweep of the same day, after every footer was read in full. `BF-73` is the hiss and the crows; `BF-74`–`BF-76` are the three score designs of the same day; `BF-77`–`BF-80` are the frame at both ends of every chapter, ordered by the author on 2026-11-12 and built by `tools/build_frame.py`; `BF-81` is the first glance and the jealousy in Chapter 19, also his order, of the same day; `BF-92` is the fifth stop seen from the accounts office, minted on the Chapter 9 sweep of 2026-11-14.

## How this register is kept

1. **One line the day it is made.** A chapter may not close without its butterflies in this file, and the footer key is cited in the entry.
2. **Numbered, never renumbered.** A withdrawn butterfly keeps its number and is marked `WITHDRAWN` with the reason — `BF-11` and Chapter 16's message in her lap are the models.
3. **The cost is written down**, not only the act: what a butterfly does to a later chapter belongs in the entry.
4. **Everything else is not a butterfly.** A restored canon line, a corrected name or an object placed in a room is a correction of the page, and belongs in the chapter's `FIXED:`/`RESTORED:` key, not here.
