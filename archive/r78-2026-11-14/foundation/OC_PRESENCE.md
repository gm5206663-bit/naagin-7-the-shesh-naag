# OC PRESENCE — how Vihaan is woven in

**Owns: the law of his presence, and the map of where he stands in each episode.** `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` owns who he is; `foundation/ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` owns what he can do; this page owns the one thing that went wrong on 2026-11-11 — a whole chapter written without him.

## 1 · What happened, and what it was

Chapter 4 was written faithful to canon and with no Vihaan in it. The chapter was legal: every camera gate green, every canon beat whole. It was also **not this serial**. The author's word for it: *"serious problem, oc integration, naturally everything, and others many things is missing... a serious very obvious mistake."*

The cause was a rule doing the work of a law. `WRITING.md` §"Him" asks, scene by scene, *is he in this scene? Only if he would be* — a good question with no floor under it, and I answered it scene by scene until the answer for the whole chapter came out **no**. A scene may exclude him. **A chapter may not.**

## 2 · The law

1. **Floor.** Every chapter has him in **at least two** places, and each one has a **cause the reader can see** (why he is standing there) and a **consequence** (what his being there changes for the reader, not for canon).
2. **Ceiling.** He never takes a canon line, a canon action, or a canon decision from the family. He is not in a scene canon needs to be about someone else — or if he is, he is on the edge of it, handling its furniture.
3. **Never as the help — corrected 2026-11-11, on the author's word.** The earlier version of this rule sent him into canon's rooms with his hands full: *he holds the ladder, carries the cable, drives the car, is sent for things.* The author read what that produced and named it: *"what the hell this even vihaan doing."* It turned a son of that house into a man doing the night repairs, and it turned the narrator into a coincidence — wherever the plot needed a useful pair of hands, there he was with a spool of cable.
   **The rule now:** his reason to be in a room is **never a job**. It is one of four things — **he lives there**, **he works there**, **the family wants something from him** (an errand a son is given, not one the staff are given), or **he was already there** when the scene arrived. Physical labour may befall him as that house's ordinary contempt, **once**, as a fact somebody sees, and never as the plot's excuse to place him. **If a scene needs a pair of hands, the hands belong to a servant.**
4. **He is not a narrator of canon.** He does not see the things the reader is meant to keep from the house. He sees the *damage*: a wet floor, a dented bumper, a car that comes home late. What he does with it is nothing, for now — and that nothing is the story.
5. **Ignorance is not innocence by accident.** Whatever he does not know, the page must be able to say why he does not know it: not invited, not told, sent out, asleep, away.
6. **No knowledge he has not earned** (`O015`). No dream, no instinct that names things, no line that shows he suspects what he is (`foundation/SHESHVEER.md` §3).
7. **He is seen, not only used** (`O025`). Hands carrying a tray are not a person. Every chapter puts his face and body on the page at least twice, by somebody else's eyes — a room turning, a sister saying it out loud, a stranger looking twice — and never by the narration praising him and never by a line that disclaims his looks. `WRITING.md` §5a is the method; `tools/oc_check.py` OC6 and OC7 enforce the floor.
8. **He has a life the chapter is not about, told in this show's terms** (`O026`). A school in England, a college, four years at the family's London desk, the flat above it, the wedding season that brought him home. It shows only in what he can do and in what he does not say. **No foreign cast, no career abroad, no city of his own** — invented material passes the same test as canon's: could this show say it.

9. **He is heard, not only seen.** A chapter that puts him in three or more scenes owes him **at least two lines of his own**, and no scene may make his silence his part in it unless the footer says why (`SILENT:`). This is not the same law as law 2: a man on the edge of a canon room handling its furniture is still a man who *speaks* to the people in it — he runs floors, he answers a joke with an answer, he changes a room number in front of the whole hall because he has thought of the east window. **The third occurrence, 2026-11-12:** Chapter 19 gave him nine scenes and four lines, and closed its biggest moment on *"he says nothing to anybody."* The author's sentence on it: *"Vihaan should talk or others things, you make him silent, that is completely wrong."* Discipline about what he may not know (`O034`) was taken as permission to give him nothing to say, and that is the error this law exists to stop (`O040`; `WRITING.md` §0.1; `tools/oc_check.py` OC9–OC10).

**Under `L-23` (2026-11-11).** Three causes in the map above are **labour** causes — the coil of flex in Ch 3, the light stand in Ch 4's steps, the lens carried down in Ch 6 — and the rule now says a job is never the reason he is in a room. **Chapter 9's two are already withdrawn** (a spool of cable in the tenth-floor passage, eight chairs in the hall). *Rows ending "to be placed" are decided when that chapter is written, not later, and never silently.*

## 4 · The gate

`tools/oc_check.py chapters/<file>` reads the scenes and fails a chapter that has him nowhere, that gives him fewer than two lines across three or more scenes (**OC9**), or that writes his silence for him without a reason on the footer's `SILENT:` line (**OC10**). It prints, per chapter: *in N scene(s), with M line(s) the page gives him, and K scene(s) written mute* — so law 1's count and law 9's count can both be checked against this map by eye. `--selftest` plants all ten faults.

**Backfill owed:** Chapters 1, 2 and 3 were checked against this law on 2026-11-11 and pass (he is in each: the cot; the gate; the ladder, the corridor, the roof). Chapter 4 failed and was rewritten the same day, from 2,938 words to 3,478.

**The second correction, same day:** laws 7 and 8 were added after the author asked what he could possibly have been doing in London and why nobody ever described his face. All five chapters were revised for both: every one now has somebody looking at him (OC6), and his past is built behind him (`OC_VIHAAN.md` §3a). The gate's own summary line now reads: *every chapter has him in at least two scenes, says where, and somebody looks at him.*

## 5 · The second occurrence, and the law that came out of it

**Chapter 10, 2026-11-11.** Written from `canon/E04_BEATS.md`, 3,498 words, thirteen scenes, `gap_check.py` **29 of 29 caption cues on the first pass** — and **Vihaan in it nowhere.** Not at the gate, not on the steps for the family photograph, not in the lane when a child comes off a balcony two houses from his own. The author's words: *"you completely and mostly putting aside vihaan, what the hell, you forceing canon."*

The cause is in §1 and it is worse the second time. Chapter 4 failed because a rule did the work of a law. Chapter 10 failed because **I ran the tools that count canon and not the one that counts him** — `check.py`, `gap_check.py`, `recount.py`, and never `oc_check.py`. `oc_check.py` was on disk, it takes one argument, and it went red the first time it was asked. The 100% was reported to the author in the same breath as the chapter, as a virtue.

**What is now law (`L-24`).**

1. **`oc_check.py` runs before `gap_check.py`**, on every new chapter, every time. Coverage is a fact-check; his presence is the serial.
2. **A chapter declares the scene that is not canon's**, in the footer, on an `OURS:` line: the one place the page exists because this story is being told, not because a cue was logged. Chapter 10's is the lane — he picks a key up out of gravel and closes a neighbour's question with a sentence about a party — and it is the only reason the chapter is in this house rather than in the episode.
3. **Where he stands in Chapter 10.** First written as **two places** — and the author's next word was *"still not enough, things should be naturally."* Correct: two blocks that can be lifted out whole are a placement, not a presence. He is now in **nine touches across the party, the gate, the lane and the veranda** — eight since 2026-11-14, when the glass at the pillar came off this list — and `tools/oc_check.py` reads the chapter as four scenes declared in the footer, 31 places he stands, ten lines he speaks, seen in three paragraphs. They are threaded on one private fact that has nothing to do with the plot — **there is a flight in the morning** (*kal subah ki flight*). **No page in this book names an hour.** The six o'clock this line and Chapter 10's footer used to carry came out of *Chapter 11's* first draft — `HISTORY.md` l.787, *the bag set down against the wall — his flight is at six* — a line the visual pass of 2026-11-12 cut along with that chapter's ending, leaving the hour in three files and on no page. Both footers and this line were corrected on 2026-11-14 to what the prose will carry:
   - the doorway, where his father presents him to Tina's father the way a man shows a car;
   - his mother's hands at his collar;
   - a neighbour asking whose boy he is, and being told *Vihaan. London se aaya hai*;
   - Aryaman using him as the butt of a joke in front of the men, and him saying nothing;
   - his mother's hand closing on his sleeve through the ring ceremony;
   - the half-step back when Aryaman leaves the floor; **the glass of water at the pillar is struck from this list (2026-11-14)** — the pillar and the full glass are Chapter 9's, twice in its passage (`Chapter_09` l.182, l.184) and once at the bar (`l.358`), on the same night, and `STATUS.md` §41 already carries the law that the glass in his hand stays full at every party. Chapter 10 was a third copy of one gesture in one evening, which is repetition, not presence;
   - **the out-house gate**, on the phone in English, ending the call to step aside for a girl carrying two bags — which is also why he is there at all when the child comes off the balcony;
   - the key out of the gravel, one look at a wrist, and the neighbour's question closed;
   - **the corner of the veranda**, where he sees two sisters on the grass and goes back inside.

   The old two, kept only as the shape of the first attempt:
   - **The photograph on the steps.** His mother's hands at his collar; a woman from Tina's side looking at him twice and asking whose boy he is; the back row where the veranda's light does not reach.
   - **The lane, after the child.** Out of his own brother's function; the rail off its plaster; the key out of the gravel with the dirt on it still; a wrist he looks at once and does not ask about. (Written back into the prose at `Chapter_10` l.504 on 2026-11-14 — until that day this bullet described a scene the chapter did not have, which is the exact fault `BF-91` counts.) **He does not see the change**, and his not being told is why he is safe in this story.
