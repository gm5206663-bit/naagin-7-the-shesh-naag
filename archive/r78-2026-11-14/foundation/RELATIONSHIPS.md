## 1 · Canon ties at the opening of 2025

**The Aroras**
- Ananta and Purvi: the centre of that house. Ananta shields her, fights her battles, dresses her, promises her the job. Purvi worships her: "you are a genius; nobody looks at me." Ananta: "you are my *jaan*."
- Gurinder and Shalini carry the secret of the swapped names. Neither daughter knows.
- Rohan mocks Purvi; Ananta goes after him for it.
- Ananta Arora and Ravish Suri are lovers. It is not yet known in either house. (From Episode 3, where she tells him she is carrying his child. Episode 1 does not show it.)

**The Suri house**
- Aryaman and "Parmeet": "Thank you, Dad." Aryaman's whole loyalty. On the other side, a use and a date.
- Ravish is "Parmeet's" own son (R13). Karan and Arjun are Baljeet's. Aryaman has voted-for, fought-for brothers here: "I have always held him my brother and I always will" (Ravish, C, Ep 24 06:23).
- Aryaman and his sisters, Pinky and Nisha: the people he is soft with. They dress his newsroom like a mandap because he never goes out.
- Karan does not much like Aryaman. Arjun follows Karan and is close to Nisha.
- Aryaman and Radhika: she left him on their wedding day. He drinks and calls marriage suicide.
- The house and Airul: fear.

**The naags**
- Uttara and Bharani watch the Arora house and wait for their queen.
- The cursed family watches too, unseen by everyone.

**Across the houses**
- Ananta Arora works for the Suri channel. That is the only tie. Gurinder has not yet met "Parmeet".

### 3b · What stands between him and Purvi (`O029`)

Not a rival. Two facts that are one fact:

1. **Destiny names a king, and the king is him.** Naagraj and Naagrani are meant for each other; the man she is destined to is *Veer, King of Naaglok*; that is Vihaan, and **nobody in this world knows it, himself included.** Every scene in which the destiny is spoken — Uttara, the temple, the kul's law — is the obstacle named aloud to her while the man it names stands next to her.
2. **To almost everyone, he is human.** Canon forbids a naagin to love a human and calls the punishment heavy; the Anant kul's curse lifts only when the Naagrani marries a naag. So the kul sees her love as the sin and the destiny as the thing that will take her away from him — and the kul is wrong on both counts, and no argument will fix it.

**Almost nobody knows his name** (`O032`). It is **Anantveer**; the short form belongs to almost no one; Parmeet does not know it and neither does anybody in that house. So the ignorant ones help without knowing it, and the enemy who knows, if there is one, has to come from outside (`OPEN-18`).

**And the kul does not try to marry her to the king** (`O030`): there is no king to marry her to, because he is unawakened and unknown. So the law has no bridegroom and lands on the human instead — the opposition is to **Vihaan**, and it is a family's fear, not a plot. Because the pair together cannot be beaten, anybody who means to win must keep them apart; and because the world already believes he is human, that weapon is free.

Canon's own track stays whole: she comes to love Aryaman too, and resolves to love in silence (`RELATIONSHIPS` §2). That is not erased to make room. It is the third wall of the same trap — and two of its three walls are the same man.

### 3a · What the years away made of him (`O026`)

Eleven years in England, told in this show's terms and no others: a school, a college, the family's small London desk, the flat above it, and the wedding season that brought him home.

- **Work:** the family's London end in his hands from twenty-two — the office, the property, the money that crosses borders — and better run than it had been. He is not a son who has been kept; he is a son who has been **used, well, and far away**, which is how this family keeps someone out of the room without insulting him.
- **Money:** his own, earned by running the family's London end, and more of it than anyone in that house assumes. He does not talk about money, which in this family is remarkable in itself.
- **Women:** there have been some; they are not the story (`O017`). He is not waiting for anyone and has not been wounded by anyone. When Purvi looks at him she is not the first to look, and the page must never treat her look as the event the world was holding its breath for.
- **What he left:** a flat, a desk, a feed that runs at odd hours — all of it given up without regret. He came home to stay, as far as he knows.
- **What it costs him in the house:** he has never been in the room when this family decided anything, and he does not read that as an injury, which is exactly what makes it one.

## 4 · The romance (`O004`)

**The rulings:** between the female lead and Vihaan; it comes naturally, among the other things of the story (`O004`). She loves him in the first arc (`O016`). The author does not impose it: "it's not something i put it was naturally should happen"; it follows from who the two truly are, from canon's own law, and from where they stand (`O017`).

**Two pairs of eyes, and the pull before the awakening (`O033`).** The attraction is in the room from the first time they share one, in both of them, before any awakening and before either has a reason; it is never named by them and never explained by the narration (`canon/STYLE_CANON.md` §1g). And the world and the pair do not see the same thing: to that house **Purvi is not a beautiful girl** — the plain one in spectacles beside a sister everybody turns for — and to **him** she is beautiful at once, and more than beautiful, *something special* he has no word for. Her eyes on him are the other half: the world already finds him beautiful (`O025`), so what she sees that nobody else does is not his face but the quiet in him that she cannot look at for long. **Nothing corrects the gap** — no makeover, no compliment from the room, no narration taking a side — and it is spent one device to a chapter (`canon/TWO_PAIRS_OF_EYES.md` §4).

**What "naturally" is taken to mean here**
1. It is not announced, planned in the narration, or helped by coincidence. They meet where canon puts them both.
2. It grows from what each one does in front of the other, in scenes that are about something else.
3. Each has a life the other is not in. She has her sister, her forgetting, her wish to be a journalist, and a temple calling her. He has a house he is relearning.
4. Canon's feelings are real and are not erased to make room. If she admires Aryaman, she admires him. What changes, changes by cause.
5. Neither is written as the other's reward.
6. Nothing is declared before the people themselves would know it.
7. The pace is the author's. A step that cannot be taken back (a confession, a kiss, a promise) is not written without his word.
8. **A pull is not love.** That people notice him, that she does, is not trust, forgiveness or consent, and is never written as if it were. He earns her trust by distinct things he does.
9. **Her scenes stay hers:** her grief, her revenge, her wits, her kills. He does not solve them for her.
10. **Her will is shown before any touch.** She chooses, visibly, first.
11. **Aryaman is never made small** so that Vihaan can stand taller. He stays the man the show made him.
12. **Neither of them is told they are meant for each other.** The curse's law (`OPEN-07`) is under the story; it is not a reason either of them has.

(8 to 11 are the author's own rules for his other naag serial and for his Supergirl serial, taken as method: `Sara/UNDERSTANDING.md`.)

**What stands in its way, from canon** (none of this is to be smoothed over)
- She is a naagin and loving a human is forbidden to her (C, Ep 44 04:10); Uttara and Bharani will act against it, and in canon they do it by wearing the loved man's face (R10). They think he is human. So does he.
- He is a Suri. After Episode 7 that is the name of the people who killed her family.
- He was not there that night (`O003`). She does not know it: the one who stabbed her wore his face and spoke as him (`O016`; canon plays it with Aryaman's, C Ep 7 22:34). In canon she believes it of Aryaman until Bharani owns to it in Episode 17 (digest; to be read in the captions when that ledger is built).
- Bharani struck to stop her loving a human (C, Ep 15 02:36). The man she struck for is a naag, and nobody knows it, he least of all.
- Canon gives her marriage to Aryaman (`OPEN-06`), and Aryaman is a good man.

**What speaks for it, from canon**
- The curse lifts when the Naagrani marries a naag (`OPEN-07`).
- His parents were killed by the same men, on their own night, some days before she was born. Neither knows.
