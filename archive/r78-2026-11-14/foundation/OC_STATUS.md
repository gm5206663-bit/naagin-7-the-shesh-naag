## 2 · Natural talents — what twenty-four years of integration made of him

Source for all six: the Adaptation Talent, present since conception, never idle, doing one piece of work — making a naag of the Shesh line whole inside a human life. **It is not a voice, not a menu, not a feeling of being helped, and he has no name for it** (`ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` §4; lock 2). None of these is supernatural and none may be written as if it were.

| Talent | State | Scope, exactly | Where it is on the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** — strength, speed, balance, endurance | 5 · ON THE PAGE | The best a human frame gives and not beyond it. Lean, quick and supple together; never bulk. | Ch4, the light stand and the falling lens caught one-handed at the bottom of the channel's steps; Ch2, the bag and the stairs |
| **Learning by watching** — a skill seen once, kept | 5 · ON THE PAGE | Human skills only, and only what he has actually been shown. It does not hand him knowledge he was never given. | Ch4, the camera bay: *"You have done this before"* before he has said a word about it |
| **Settling in** — climate, food, sleep, manners, speech, any company | 5 · ON THE PAGE | He is at home wherever he is put, at once, without a scene about it. | Ch2, the house he has not lived in for eleven years, entered without a beat of strangeness; Ch4, a newsroom at one in the morning |
| **Reading people** — what a room feels and wants | 5 · ON THE PAGE | Moods and wants only. **It bends at the one place he does not look hard: the people he loves.** His family has had twenty-four years of practice and he has no reason to doubt them. | Ch4, *he shouts at everyone on Monday, except the ones he likes*; Ch7, told where his father is by the woman who will be blamed for telling him |
| **Presence** — a face and bearing a room notices | 5 · ON THE PAGE | The human ceiling and a high one. **It compels nothing.** It cannot make anyone consent, trust or love him, and no line on the page is allowed to say so in a way that shrinks him (`O025`, `WRITING.md` §5a). | Ch2 the hall going quiet; Ch4 the photographers on the wall; Ch6 the lift; Ch7 Ananta at the lift, twice |
| **Health** — and a healthy man's healing at its best | 5 · HELD | No resistance to venom, drugs, fire or blade. Never once ill on the page, which is all it means. | Nowhere yet, and it is not to be made a plot point before the awakening |

**Not talent, and not to be filed as talent:** the ignorance he lives in (`OC_PRESENCE.md` §2 rule 5 makes the page say why he does not know a thing), and the pull the pearl has toward him, which is the pearl's behaviour and not his ability (§5 below).

### 4b · What he has now

**Nothing.** Not a sense, not a reflex, not a healing, not a dream that names something. He walks into the Naag Mandir's world every day and it is only a world. This is the one lock that outranks every other entry on this page:

> **No supernatural act, sense or resistance before the awakening. Integration is not expression.** (`ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` lock 1)

Two things sit close to the line and both stay on the human side of it:

- **Ch6, the lift floor coming up warm.** He noticed it, and noticing is human. It is the whole of what he has, and the page says nothing about why (`STATUS_PANEL.md`, delta for Chapter 6).
- **The house's ignorance of him, and his of it.** Nobody in the story knows he is a naag; he does not know it either. That is not an ability; it is the state of the board.

### 4c · What the awakening gives him, by law (`OPEN-15`, `O021`, `O022`)

Not a gift arriving. **A body remembering a rank it never learned** — capacity, never memory. It is written as guardianship, not attack, and here is the whole of its approved shape:

| Comes back | Shown as |
|---|---|
| **Shield** | He puts himself between a blow and a person, and it holds |
| **Sense** | He knows a naagin is near him — not who, not why, not what she is |
| **Hold shut** | A door, a lid, a thing that must not open, kept closed by him |
| **Take a blow meant for someone else** | He bears what was aimed at another, and it costs him |

**What it does not give him:** technique; the history of his kind; any knowledge at all; control that is skill rather than instinct; a word for what has happened to him (`ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` §6). He wakes **in pain, alone, far from that house, believing he is dying** (`OPEN-14`), on the night the woman he was born beside is stabbed wearing his face (`O016`) — and knows nothing.

**He does not kill any of the people her revenge is owed to.** His power is real, it is not a shortcut, and it never takes a scene from her (gate 17; `STORY.md` §1 law 8). If a scene needs a death, that death is hers or canon's.

### 4d · Closed for good (`SHESHVEER.md`)

Not powers he lacks — powers this season is never allowed to explain or grant:

- **Any memory of the old life.** Not at the awakening, not later, not at the end (`O022`).
- **Any technique or duty of the Maha Naag Raj** beyond the pearl's guardianship.
- **Any connection between his old life and Purvi's line.** If one exists it is the author's to reveal.
- **Any suggestion of another world.** One world, one mystery, no meta.
- **Any act that names the pearl's origin, or the woman of that life.**

What is allowed instead, and is not his power at all: other people's fear, the pearl's behaviour, one word from the old world misplaced, and the five hoods seen once in the dark (`SHESHVEER.md` §2).

## 5 · The pearl, which is not an ability

`O022`: **"the Naagmani knows him very well, too much very well."** This is a standing fact about him, not a skill he can use: it moves toward him, refuses a hand he is standing near, gives light not meant for the one holding it. **It is never explained, never named by him, and never a line he says** (`SHESHVEER.md` §2).

And the counterweight that keeps the mystery alive: **Airul reads it wrong.** He concludes that Vihaan Suri is a marked *doorkeeper* of the pearl, a human born to carry it, and every move he makes toward him follows from that error (`OPEN-17`). Vihaan is therefore wanted alive, by the wrong man, for the wrong reason.

## 6 · What he may never do, in one list

Five locks from `ADAPTATION_MODULE.md` §7 and four from `SHESHVEER.md` §3, kept here so a scene can be checked in one place:

1. No supernatural act, sense or resistance before the awakening.
2. The Talent is never a voice, a system, or a feeling of guidance.
3. A gain, once settled, is not forgotten; a first exposure from outside is never painless.
4. He is never weakened to keep a canon scene, never made foolish to produce one, and never strengthened to win one.
5. Nothing from Sheshveer — no name, no memory, no echo dressed as instinct.
6. No canon line, action or decision taken from a member of that family.
7. No knowledge he has not earned (`O015`): no dream, no premonition, no convenient suspicion.
8. Nothing that makes him small: not the errand, not the tray, not the pretty one who is harmless (`O026`; gate 22).
9. Nothing that takes a scene from her (`O024`; gate 17).

## 7 · Where it stands after Chapter 7

- **Nothing new.** No power, no sense, no gain. The awakening is ahead, on the night of Episode 7, and the calendar has not moved him.
- **On the page, in his three scenes:** the London month-end waited at a shut door; the drive with the bucket and the neem tree, where the men tell him the drunk's story and call him *Chhote sahab*; the hall, where Purvi holds up new spectacles that no longer suit her eyes. All three are §2 and §3 abilities, and the chapter footer declares them.
- **The record-room file is in her hands, not his.** He is the reason it exists in that building, and he is not in the room where it is delivered — the gap is declared in the footer, and it is the pattern of the whole season so far: **he sees the damage, never the secret** (`OC_PRESENCE.md` §2 rule 4).
- **What he still does not have:** any knowledge, any teacher, any clan, any word for what he is. His own body is the only evidence, and it says nothing.
