# Sheshveer — what may be on the page

**Owns: the boundary around what Vihaan was — what is fixed by the author, what may be shown on the page, and what is closed to this season no matter how good the scene would be.** Read it before writing him, and before letting any character explain him. Canon ground is in `canon/FRANCHISE.md`; his present life is in `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md`.

The author's ruling (`O021`, `O022`): Vihaan is the rebirth of **Sheshveer**, a Sheshnaag, golden, five-hooded, who held the rank of **Maha Naag Raj** in his own right and was the **Naagmani's** protector. **No memory ever comes back.** The old life is finished — the original completed his destiny. And in that older life he stood beside a woman: the partner and husband of the female lead of another story the author is writing.

That last sentence is the reason this page exists. **Sheshveer is the author's multiverse, and Season 7 is not where it gets explained.** He is building other work around that figure; here he must remain mostly mystery. So this file is a firewall, not a character sheet. It says what is fixed, what may be *seen* in Season 7, and what is **closed** to this season's page no matter how tempting.

## 2 · What the reader may be shown in Season 7

Mystery is not silence. Mystery is a room where something is obviously in the corner and nobody turns the light on. Allowed:

1. **Other people's reaction to what he is.** Fear, deference, a word bitten off. Airul knowing more than he says. A naagin taking one step back without deciding to.
2. **The pearl's behaviour.** It knows him. It can move toward him, refuse to be lifted by a hand he is standing near, or give light that is not for the person holding it. Never with an explanation attached.
3. **What his body does and cannot do.** Capacity without knowledge (`OPEN-15`): he shields; he senses; something in him is old and does not say so.
4. **The five hoods, once, in the dark, with no one to name them.** A shape seen, counted by the reader, not by a character.
5. **One word from the old world, misplaced.** Uttara, Bharani or the been may say a fragment. Not a lecture. Not a history.

## 3 · Closed to this season's page

These are the questions Season 7 must leave standing. If any of them gets answered here, the author's other work loses its ground.

- **What the *original* Sheshveer was at the end of his own story.** The new one's titles are given; the old one's ending is not, and the gap is deliberate (`O023`): an echo tells the reader nothing about the voice. This is the most important line on this page.
- **How Sheshveer died**, and whether he chose it.
- **What the rank demanded** beyond the pearl, and whom the Maha Naag Raj ruled.
- **The woman of that life** — the other story's female lead. She is never named, never described, never half-drawn here. Not a memory, not a resemblance, not a "she looked like".
- **The Naagmani's origin and what it did to him.** The pearl knows him; *why* is not this season's business.
- **The connection between his old life and Purvi's line.** If one exists, it is the author's to reveal, not mine.
- **Any suggestion that the multiverse exists on the page.** No other worlds, no other births, no meta. In Season 7 there is one world and one mystery.

## 4 · Where the mystery pays off instead

Not in information — in **cost**. He wakes as the guardian of a pearl he does not know he guarded, on the night the woman he was born beside was stabbed wearing his face (`O016`), and he remembers nothing — ever. That is the whole of it, and it is enough for a season: the reader can see what he is long before he can, and what he can never have is the reason he is worth reading.

## 5 · The one place I overrule the author's own comfort, and why

`O022` says "nothing is absolute, you can make things accordingly when necessary." So, on gold: canon keeps golden for the born Naagrani — no other kechuli is golden, "theirs is white or colourless" (C, Ep 33 00:11:30) — and a naagin's golden scent is what drives the dragon (C, Ep 3 00:30:51). I therefore wrote his **hoods** gold and his **kechuli** white, so the pair carries one mark in two places and Purvi's line stays true as spoken. If he wants the whole serpent gold, he says so and I will write it, but then Episode 33's line has to be handled rather than ignored.
