**Owns: Vihaan's Adaptation Talent in this world, in the seven parts of §71 of the master foundation.** It sits beneath the locked universal core (the author's `adaptation-talent` repository: `MASTER-FOUNDATION.md`, `LEVEL-FRAMEWORK.md`) and rewrites none of it. Where this file and the core disagree, the core is right and this file is corrected.

**Created:** 2026-11-11, on `O009` and `O011`. The consolidated register this module feeds, with every talent, skill and power in one table and its state named, is `OC_STATUS.md`. **Revised the same day** after the author's other records were read (`O018`; `Sara/UNDERSTANDING.md`). The first version called him ordinary before the awakening. That undersold him and misread the doctrine.

**How "unawakened" is read.** The Talent is present from conception and is never idle. What has not happened yet is expression. For twenty-four years the Talent has been doing one piece of work: integrating what he is, a naag of the Shesh line living a human life, into one whole. In the author's own words for his other naag holder, everything before the awakening is "integration, not expression", and the holder reads as "extraordinary-but-human". Nothing supernatural shows, and nobody, he least of all, sees a cause. His own record for this serial says it in one line: the Talent is "From womb, unrecognized", and shows as "Unconscious 'giftedness,' dormant until naag awakening" (`GENESIS_CODEX.md`, the holders table). This is the universal doctrine applied to him; no person, event or number of the other naag project crosses over (`O012`).

## 1 · Identity (§71.1)

- **Holder:** Vihaan Suri. Born to human parents; a naag of the Shesh line (`O011`; how, `OPEN-08`). Does not know either fact.
- **Universe / branch:** *Naagin* season seven, with him in it.
- **Timeline / checkpoint:** before Chapter 1. Early 2025, the day he lands from London, where he was sent at thirteen (`OC_VIHAAN.md` §3a).
- **Age / stage:** twenty-four, close to twenty-five. Adult; unawakened.
- **Starting conditions, honestly:** a body and mind the Talent has had twenty-four years to make whole; a school and a college in England; the family's London end run by him since he was twenty-two, and run better than it had been; money of his own; a camera he works as well as anyone in that family; a rich house that is not his; a family that means him no good; no teacher, no knowledge of naags, no clan; a birthright he cannot yet reach. One thing in his favour that he does not know about: the law of this world asks for a naag at the Naagrani's side (`OPEN-07`, confirmed by `O017`).

## 2 · Local reality (§71.2)

What is true in this world, as the episodes give it (`STORY.md` §3 holds the full list with tags).

- **Naags and naagins** are born, not made. An ichchhadhari naagin takes any shape, human or snake, and other people's faces. Poison, speed, strength, a great serpent form, a five-headed form for the queen. A male of the kind says of himself, "I too am an ichchhadhari naag" (C, Ep 44).
- **The opening comes late and by the moon.** Purvi shows nothing until near twenty-four; then moonlight forces the first change, painfully, and further "phases" follow.
- **Rules that bind them:** a blow to the spine and then a brass dagger, while the been plays, kills a naagin (R19); on Poornima a naagin must shed her skin and is at her weakest; mirrors and water can show her true form; a sapera's been forces the change; a naagin can wipe a human's memory, at the cost of penance and a rare sea herb.
- **Dragons** are the old enemy, equal in strength, cold-blooded, red-eyed, bound to a pearl, hurt by the damru's sound, fed by sunlight.
- **The Naagmani** is Mahadev's, kept by the Naagrani; on a forehead it can wipe a memory or wake stone.
- **Mahadev's word is law:** the curse, its lifting, who may marry whom.

**Not available here:** cultivation, ranks, energy trained from nothing, systems, panels a character can see. No human becomes a naag by effort. No one learns shape-changing by watching it.

## 3 · Starting adaptation (§71.3)

- **Prenatal and childhood:** the integration began before birth and has never stopped. Nothing of it was ever seen as strange: a child who was seldom ill, learned at once, and was at home wherever he was put.
- **Adaptive Order and maturity:** **Order I — Foundational.** Mature in everything a human life has asked of him.
- **What it has made of him, as results and never as a named cause:**
  - a body at the top of what a man's can be without anyone thinking it more than that: lean, strong, quick and supple together, never bulk;
  - **a face and body at the top of the human scale, and people looking** (`O025`). It stops sentences and starts nothing. What it cannot do — make anyone consent, trust or love him — is shown by events and never announced in a line that apologises for it (`WRITING.md` §5a);
  - health that has never failed him, and a healthy man's healing at its best;
  - a mind that learns a skill from seeing it done, keeps what it learns, and settles into a new place, language or company at once;
  - a steadiness under pressure that others read as calm.
- **What is not there yet:** anything a naag can do. No venom, no change of shape, no sense for his own kind, no resistance to venom, been, fire or blade. He can be drugged, deceived and killed like any man.

## 6 · Progression path (§71.6) — laws, never plot

- **Now:** integration continues. Nothing new is entering.
- **The awakening:** at about twenty-five (`O011`). In this world the moon near a birthday opens a naagin; what opens him, and the form he takes, are the author's (`OPEN-09`).
- **What the doctrine says of that moment:** what has been integrated for twenty-four years is his at once and sits safely in him. It does not have to be grown from nothing, and his own nature is not a danger to him.
- **What it does not give him:** technique, the history of his kind, or any knowledge at all. Control by instinct is not skill. He will not know what he is doing or what it means.
- **After it:** a first meeting with anything from outside (venom, a been, a dragon's fire, a brass blade) is still dangerous the first time. What he meets again he bears better, each gain with its scope. A gain once settled changes what he perceives, what is hard for him and how he acts, and it does not fade between chapters. If he is ever overwhelmed, the excess that did it can be named.
- **Bottlenecks:** no teacher; no knowledge; a house that would kill him if it knew; the first of his kind he meets have reason to fear a Suri.
- **Shortcuts that do not exist:** watching a naagin change does not teach him to change. The Talent does not lend him her powers, tell him things, or act on cue when a scene needs it. Nothing arrives before its cause.
