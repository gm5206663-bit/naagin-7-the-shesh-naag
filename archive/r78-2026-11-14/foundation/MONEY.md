# MONEY — what a rupee is worth in this serial

**Owns: the size of money in this book, and the figures the prose is allowed to say.** `tools/money_check.py`
reads this file and holds every chapter to it. The reason it exists is the author's line of 2026-11-13,
*“₹350-crore is not enough money that he needed to leave”*: the figure was not too big to be interesting,
it was **not ours** — `Sara/SARA.md` §2476's business, pasted here. `L-25` says a fact that lives in one
file nobody reads as canon is a fact nobody decided; this file is where money stops being invented in
passage and starts being decided.

---

## 1 · The ceiling

**Ceiling: ₹6.2 crore.** The broadcast feed and the screens for the Kumbh, fifteen days on the mela
ground, sold through the London end — the largest single paper in the season, and this book's largest,
because the channel is a regional one (`foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` §3b). Nothing in a scene may be bigger
than that. A number above the ceiling is not scale, it is another serial.

## 3 · The two house months, and the one figure

**₹24 lakh (Ch11) and ₹9.40 lakh (Ch18) are both true, and the difference is the point.** Ch18's figure is
the show's: what a house of that size spends in a month. Ch11's is ours: the Suris' month, and the list
attached to it in the prose — guards, cars, three new men, a tent nobody mentioned — is what makes it two
and a half times the first. Nobody is to "reconcile" them by changing either number; if a later chapter
compares them, it compares them the way the sentence above does.

**One figure runs the season: forty lakh.** It is the price of the bigha, the value of the necklace, the
sum the house cannot reach, and the answer to Dimple's *toh chalo khareed lete hain*. It is not a fortune;
it is pressure, which is what `BF-88` decided.

## 4 · Struck, and why — recorded here so they stay dead

Struck from `foundation/OC_VIHAAN.md` §3b on 2026-11-13: **₹350 crore** (the Kumbh contract as first
written), **₹40 crore** (the annual sponsorship line), **₹12 crore**, **₹3.2 crore**, **₹1.65 crore**,
**₹80 lakh**, **₹1.5 crore**, **₹15 crore** (the Meridian number, the one that made a gala the largest
paper of his year). All of them were either the other serial's money or an invented scale, and none of
them is in the column above — which is how the gate treats them: any chapter that says one is RED with
`MONEY-2`, and this section cannot re-legitimise a figure by recording it.

## 5 · What the gate enforces

`tools/money_check.py`, from `tools/check.py` as check 8. `MONEY-1` a figure above the ceiling;
`MONEY-2` a figure not in the column — either write the row with what it buys, or change the number;
`MONEY-3` a row in the column that no chapter says any more, which is the ledger and the book parting
company; `MONEY-4` a money-shaped figure the word list could not read, printed as a note and never red.
`--scan` prints every figure it found, with the line number in the file. What the gate cannot check, as
always: whether a price is *worth saying in a scene* — a figure named to prove wealth is exactly the
invention `L-34` refuses.
