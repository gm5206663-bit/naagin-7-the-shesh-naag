## Part one — 2001

### B01 · Narration: the Kumbh of 2001 (01:29–02:10) · C
A narrator opens: this is the Kumbh of 2001 at Prayagraj; lakhs of people from every province come; religious heads of every faith come. This Kumbh is different: all the gurus and aghoris are being gathered for one special purpose.
- V: nothing of the story plays before 01:29. The first minute and a half is the production house's montage of temples and idols. The story opens on the Kumbh at night, a sea of lamps, with the on-screen title **"Prayagraj (Uttar Pradesh 2001)"**, followed by disclaimer cards.
- V: the man who speaks to the gathering (bearded, round glasses, beige blazer over a black polo-neck) is also shown **facing the camera** at 40:52, at the close of the episode. He is the episode's on-screen narrator as well as the professor. Whether the opening voice-over is his voice needs sound.

### B02 · The gathering in the akhara (02:17–06:45) · C, TR
The assembled gurus are restless: why were they called here, when they always meet on a fixed date in the hills? Why today in the Kumbh's akhara?
A man calms them and apologises for summoning them this way. His argument, in order:
1. "Our religions may differ, but the country is one, the blood is one, the passion is one." They have always found the answer to every calamity, including disease sent from an enemy country.
2. Whenever destructive planets align, pralay comes, and whenever the stars have signalled it they have met like this. That crisis has come again. It has not arrived yet: it will arrive **24 years from now**.
3. He explains the Kumbh: Jupiter enters Taurus after its 12-year circuit and the Sun enters Capricorn, and the Kumbh is held at the Prayagraj sangam. It is held at four places (Allahabad, Nashik, Ujjain, Haridwar). The one every 12 years at Allahabad is the Mahakumbh. After 12 of those, 144 years, comes the "Maha Mahakumbh". It is 2001 now; that one falls in **2025**. Destruction begins then: an enemy country will attack.
4. Challenged ("enemy countries always attack; how do you know today about an attack 24 years away?"), he says that **as a professor** he has researched for years; places and countries have a kundali just as people do. India's kundali told him that enemy countries jealous of India's progress will attack.
5. To prove his research he went to a **Nadi temple in South India**, where the Nadi Shastra written by Rishi Agastya is kept. The Nadi pandit confirmed it: the planetary configuration of the Mahabharata war forms again in 2025.
6. Why meet now, so many years early? Because the danger has already set foot in the country. The enemy's **infiltrators have begun putting down roots** for the attack that comes 24 years later. Reports have come from the border.

### B03 · The Suri house: Parmeet and Nimrat (07:01–08:28) · C, TR
A Punjabi-speaking couple, **Parmeet** and **Nimrat**. He hurries her packing for the Kumbh; she snaps back. Their **son's cake is still to be cut**, and they leave in the morning.
The bell rings late at night. Parmeet assumes it is "the doctor" and goes to look.
The men outside say (to each other or in aside; speaker **open**) that they crossed the border with great difficulty and will begin this country's ruin from here. To Parmeet they say they are thirsty and ask for water.
Parmeet invites them in: the weather is bad, they should eat something, his own family leaves for the Kumbh in the morning.
Then Parmeet: **"You people are not Hindustani. You are from the enemy country…"**
- TR: he notices their passports, and they murder him; Nimrat sees it and screams his name.
- V (every 2 seconds to 08:28, then every second): a house at night behind a white picket fence. A nameplate reads **"SURI"** in English and in Gurmukhi (07:02). The front room is **dressed for a birthday**: yellow bunting strung across it, balloons on the floor and the walls, a staircase behind. Nimrat, in a pale blue salwar suit, folds clothes off a hanger. Parmeet, bearded, in a lilac kurta-pyjama, is at the decorations.
- V: four or five men with bags stand at the gate. As they speak of crossing the border, one frame shows figures passing through coils of barbed wire (07:42). The one who does the talking has shaggy hair, a heavy moustache and a brown scarf. With him: a bearded man in a dark shawl, and a bald, bearded man in a red scarf. Parmeet smiles and waves them in.
- V: inside, a black bag sits on the wooden floor. A hand lifts booklets out of it (08:20). Parmeet stands reading one (08:22). His face changes (08:24–08:29). He speaks with a finger raised (08:33). **A man seizes him from behind, an arm across his throat (08:34). Nimrat, in a doorway, cries out (08:35). The shot cuts to the outside of the house (08:36) and the screen goes white (08:37).**
- **The death is not shown. What is done to Nimrat is not shown. The son does not appear in any sampled frame of the scene.** His name and age are not given. All that Episode 1 gives of him is "your son's cake" and a room dressed for a birthday.

### B04 · Who will save us (08:28–11:13) · C
Back in the akhara. The destruction has begun, but the real destruction comes in 2025. Who will save us?
"She will. She has to come." Whenever Kaal comes, Vishnu and Shiva come in some form; they will send her. If the four directions, the three worlds, the universe itself stood in her way, she would not stop.
"Neither woman nor man, neither animal nor human, neither devil nor angel nor god, but God's greatest devotee: Shiva's vardani."
An objection: **the line of Sheshnaag has ended.** Answer: a line that has Vishnu's hand and Shiva's company can never end.
Where will she be found? **"Anant kul ki Naagrani, Ananta."** She will be found **where the northern hills end, in the first lane at the edge of the city, at the house with a small Shivling outside.** Someone must go and tell her to be ready for the destruction that comes in 24 years.
"Who will go?" **"I will. I will find her."** "She is the heartbeat of Naaglok. She has every quality of a Shesh Naagin."
- The captions hear "अनंतपुर की नागरानी" once and "अनंत कुल" everywhere else. Ruling: **Anant kul**.
- V + TR: the location comes from a **bare-chested sage with a long beard and ash stripes on his forehead**, seen handing over a palm-leaf (09:00) and speaking (10:50), cut against the professor in a plain white shirt (10:30) and a Shivling in a dark blue temple (10:20). It plays as the professor's visit to the Nadi temple, inside his speech. A ruined stone temple with steps, in hills, is shown at 11:10.

### B05 · The professor and his son (11:24–12:12) · C, TR
A child: "Papa."
The professor gives him something to keep safe: "This is Papa's research. Never give it to anyone. Papa's life may be in danger." The boy begs him not to go. He must go to Ananta; by his calculation this is the right time. She must be told that ruin has knocked at the country's door.
His last lesson: **the bond with the country is the greatest bond.** If anything happens to him, the boy must remember that his father was martyred for the country, and must always put the country first.
- V: the boy looks about eight to ten, with a mop of dark curly hair. The research is a **large book with an ornate embossed metal cover**, which he hugs to his chest. The room is a study walled with books, a whiteboard, and a wall of pinned papers. The professor here wears a navy blazer over a red polo-neck, and wears it until his death.
- The son's name is **open**.
- Later canon confirms this boy is Aryaman, adopted by the house that killed his father (`CANON.md` R7). **Episode 1 does not say it.** Do not write it as known inside Episode 1, and do not name him.

### B06 · The warning on the news; the Aroras worry (12:30–13:03) · C
The address is repeated in voice-over. A news voice: infiltrators have begun entering; **people from the enemy country are entering, killing our people and taking their places.** Stay alert.
A husband to his wife **Shalini**: Parmeet and Nimrat are not answering the phone. He went to their house: it is locked. **"Who knows where that whole Suri family vanished, suddenly, without a word."** She should be careful too. She points at what the news keeps repeating.
- The husband is not named in Episode 1's captions. He is Gurinder Arora in later episodes and in the cast list.
- V: the professor drives himself, at night, in an old white car. The news plays on a boxy television in a room with ochre walls. The husband is young, in spectacles and a red check shirt; Shalini wears a pink salwar suit and a long plait.

### B07 · Tushar Sinha at the door (13:22–15:01) · C, TR
A knock. **"I am Tushar Sinha. I am a scientist. Please open the door."** Shalini does not want it opened; her husband opens it.
Tushar must meet **Ananta. "Anant kul ki Ananta."** The husband: no one of that name lives here. Tushar insists this is the address; she lives here; only she can save the country; the infiltrators have entered and only she can kill them.
He is sent away. Shalini: "I told you not to open it." Who is she? Which Ananta was he talking about? The husband, in Punjabi: how would I know.
- TR: the killers overhear this and kidnap the professor. His last captioned words outside are "Please listen to me."
- V: the moustached man from the Suri doorway sits in a car outside, watching (14:40).

### B08 · The Shesh Naagin at the door (15:01–16:40) · C, TR
Another knock. They assume it is the scientist again. It is a woman who needs help. The husband refuses: we do not help strangers. Shalini sees she is pregnant and close to delivery, and brings her in.
- TR: she is wounded and bleeding.
- V: storm clouds. She stands at the door with a grey shawl over her head, blood on her forehead, her hands joined. Shalini holds her up as the pains take her.

### B09 · The birth and the charge (16:40–17:42) · C
"It is a girl." They tell her she will be fine. She knows she will not live. They must save the child and raise her.
Why us? **"Because He wants it. It is His wish."** Who? **"Vikral. Akal. Mahakal."** Many will come to kill this child. They must save her as she, the mother, saved her today.

### B10 · The mother's account (17:42–19:36) · C, TR
In her own voice (to the child):
- She was known as the **Sarvashreshth Naagin**. She took **three births: Pratha, then Prarthana, then Pragati.**
- The greatest moment for a naagin is when her god chooses her for a purpose. She was chosen to give birth to this child, who is "more than Sarvashreshth".
- An ichchhadhari naagin is made after **100 years of tapasya. This child will be a naagin without those 100 years.**
- She is sent with Vishnu's boon, from Sheshnaag's line, with Shiva's amrit, to save India from the coming great crisis. She will be the new heir of the all-powerful **Naagmani**. She is of the **Anant kul**.
- **"I am leaving your three-year-old sister with her family, because this is the command of Shiva and Vishnu."**
- "Come, let me take you towards your destiny."
- V: the flashback is night in blue mountains. She walks alone wrapped in a shawl, a hand on her belly. **Wolves** come at her: a snarling head in close-up, then the pack (19:40–19:50). She turns and fights (20:30). TR calls them foxes; the frames show wolves, and the Episode 5 recap speaks of a wolf that once survived Pragati. **Ruling: wolves.**
- V: on the words "first Pratha, then Prarthana" there are two brief inserts: a woman in a mustard kurta with a cloth bag beside a great cobra over moonlit water (17:50), and a crowned naagin throwing golden light in a forest (17:52). They read as pictures of those two births. That reading is mine; which is which is not established.
- The three-year-old sister is not named here. Later canon names Radhika as the elder sister (Wikipedia cast list; season finale recaps). Do not name her inside Episode 1.

### B11 · The naming (20:41–20:54) · C
The mother: **"My daughter. Adbhut, alaukik, akhand, anant… Ananta."**
Shalini: "That man was saying this very name. Ananta."
- TR: the mother is fading. Her death is not captioned.
- V: at 20:54 she lies with her eyes open, blood on her face and neck, the baby against her. At 20:57–20:58 she lies still under a dark blanket and the couple stand over her, looking at each other. From 22:28 the couple hold the baby and speak as people left alone with her. No sampled frame shows her die, and none shows her afterwards. Whether she dies in that room is **open**.
- V: the birth is in the Aroras' front room. The baby lies wrapped in white beside her. She raises a finger upward as she speaks of Mahakal.

### B12 · The torture and death of Tushar Sinha (21:06–23:14) · C, TR
"Speak. Who is coming to destroy us?"
Tushar: strike a thousand times. Tomorrow she will come to answer every blow. "You can kill me. She has no end. This is Bharat, the land of Kaal Bhairav, of Mahakal. Whenever enemies like you have come, she has come."
The litany again, now complete: neither woman nor man… **Shiv vardani, Naaglok ki rani, Anant kul ki Naagrani, ichchhadhari naagin, Shesh Naagin.**
Intercut: Shalini and her husband over the baby. What will they do with her? They cannot abandon her. Look at her face. "Mata Rani, protect her."
The interrogators keep shouting "Who is coming?"
- TR: he dies. The moment of death is not captioned.
- V (every 1 to 3 seconds): a derelict room lit blue, roots or cobwebs hanging, a wooden chair. He is struck and dragged down (21:00–21:07), then held by the throat between the bald, bearded man and the shaggy, moustached man (21:10–21:11). Four men stand over him (21:34, 21:49). He lies on his side on a dusty floor, spectacles on, blood at his mouth, and goes on speaking; in several frames his mouth is wide, laughing or crying out (21:16–22:20). **At 22:50–22:51 one man bends and strikes downward while three stand behind; the next close frame of a face over him is the moustached man (22:56). From 22:52 his face lies against the floor with the eyes closed.** What he is struck with cannot be made out.
- V: the moustached man is the same figure who led the men at the Suri gate.
- V: the passage to 2025 is a dissolve from the baby to a coloured map of India.

## Part two — 2025, Delhi

### B13 · Narration: 2025 (23:14) · C
After a full 144 years the Maha Mahakumbh has come. The whole world will honour India; Prayagraj is calling the world.

### B14 · Shalini and her husband (23:14–24:17) · C, V
So much has changed: **they moved from Amritsar to Delhi.** He has not changed: the Prayagraj tickets are still not booked. He says everything is arranged. The house is too quiet: where are the girls? Both are out shopping. "Your Ananta cannot swallow a bite until she has been to the market."
- V: a carved wooden sofa with floral cushions. He is grey now, in a white kurta and mustard waistcoat, drinking tea. She wears a dark printed sari. The television shows a news channel with an **"SN" logo**, a woman anchor and pictures of the Kumbh.

### B15 · The market: Ananta and the cobra (24:40–26:55) · C, TR
A snake charmer calls his cobra out. People want the snake shut in its basket before it bites someone.
Ananta: Sheshnaag carries the whole world on his hood, and you want to shut this one in a basket? Snakes are Shiva's favourites. They are voiceless. They see what no one else can. If you keep them, look after them. "Har Har Mahadev."
A Punjabi neighbour: she has even worked magic on a snake; she is not a girl, she is a magic wand.
- TR: Ananta picks the snake up calmly and sets it by a Shivling while "Karpur Gauram" plays.
- V: a crowded, bright bazaar. Ananta in a pink-red sari with a sleeveless blouse, hair open, large earrings. The charmer wears saffron, a black headcloth and beads. A black cobra leaves its basket and crosses the cobbles. She lifts it in one hand, hood raised, and holds it up without fear (26:10–26:40). The Shivling is not in a sampled frame.

### B16 · Purvi in the market (26:55–28:14) · C, TR
The neighbour asks why her sister did not come along. A shopkeeper calls after **Purvi**: she has paid and left the jhumkas behind. "Sorry, sorry, sorry."
The neighbour: "Your sister is colder than Kailash. An auto-rickshaw in the age of the BMW."
Purvi hands over the earrings Ananta had been looking for. Ananta will wear them to the party tonight.
- Purvi falls. Captions later say boys tripped her deliberately. TR says she falls in mud.
- V: Purvi wears large dark-blue spectacles, two plaits, and layers: a brown cardigan, a green muffler. **A notepad hangs from a lanyard round her neck** (the "calendar"). She goes down in a muddy puddle in the street (27:55) and Ananta runs to her.

### B17 · Home: the forgotten tickets (28:24–30:20) · C
Ananta swears she will deal with the boys who tripped Purvi. Purvi only wants to know why **her interview call from Ananta's news channel** has not come. Ananta: it will all happen.
Their father notices Purvi's limp. Shalini asks whether Purvi booked the Mahakumbh tickets to Prayagraj. It was her job. She wears a **calendar around her neck** to remember things. She checks it: she forgot to write it down.
Their brother (Rohan in TR and the cast list): he is the favourite child, not like Purvi, "Ghajini", who forgets one thing while saying the next. A dimwit, and she wants to be a journalist.
Purvi cries. (Shalini: "Have you got a cold?" Purvi: "I am crying.") Ananta goes after the brother.
- Ananta's pet insult-endearment for Purvi is garbled in the captions. Its exact wording is **open**.
- V: the house is large and bright: brick arches, blue upholstered chairs, a long dining table.

### B18 · The watchers (30:20–31:01) · C
Ananta notices **two women** who have been standing and looking at the house for some time. When she points, they are gone.
The two, to each other: for months they have watched their Maharani; today she saw them. 2025 has come. She does not know how large a part of the great war she will be. "To the Maha Naagin of the Anant kul, our salutation."
- They are Uttara and the junior naagin (TR). The younger one is named Bharani in later episodes.
- **Dramatic irony to protect:** at this point the audience is being led to believe the Maharani is Ananta.

### B19 · The inhaler; getting ready (31:20–33:00) · C
Purvi bangs on Ananta's door: she needs her **inhaler**. She finds it.
Ananta is dressing for the **office party**. Her **boss** is about to receive journalism's biggest award for a **sting operation on a minister**; it is not announced yet but the office is sure.
Purvi: your life is so pretty. You are a genius, smart, you run the whole house, everyone looks at you. Nobody looks at me.
Ananta: you are lovely, and you are my **jaan**. Get ready. You are coming to the party. You want the job at my channel and I will get it for you.
Purvi: how? You are a **fashion designer. You make the anchors' clothes.** Why would they listen to you? You talk as if you will do magic.
Ananta: **"I don't know about magic. But I do have some superpowers."**

### B20 · The newsroom: Aryaman Suri (33:00–36:25) · C
On air: the Journalist of the Year 2025 is, for the third time running, **Aryaman Suri**. (A voice then says "four times in a row". The count is **open**.)
Aryaman wants only what is important. His assistant lists calls from the Home Minister's office, the Chief Minister's office, a message from the head of state. He wants something life-changing. She says the life-changing thing is hers: she is getting married. He calls it suicide and promises to pray for her soul.
Staff about him: no filter; he drinks all day and still never makes a mistake at work.
A colleague suggests announcing the award on the nine o'clock news. Aryaman: did your wife feed you garlic chutney last night, shall we report that too? The job is to show what is important and relevant. An award is ordinary.
**Pinky** and **Nisha** have dressed his newsroom like a wedding mandap, and brought their friends because he never goes out to meet anyone.
Family lines established in this scene:
- **Pinky** is Aryaman's own sister. **Nisha** is his cousin sister.
- **Arjun** and **Karan** are brothers to each other, and cousins. Arjun listens and is close to Nisha. Karan "gives Duryodhan vibes" and does not much like Aryaman.
- Aryaman: he is not a pizza for everyone to like. He is allergic to stupidity, to stupid people, and above all to crowds of stupid people. He loves his sisters and hates show.
- He refuses to come out to the party. Then: **"Sir, Parmeet sir and Baljeet sir have arrived."** Now he has to.

### B21 · Father and son (37:04–37:22) · C, TR
"Congratulations." "Thank you, Dad." A second man: will anyone talk to me too?
- TR: Aryaman steps out and calls Parmeet "Dad".
- **What the episode has now shown, without saying it:** a man named Parmeet was killed in 2001 by men who "take our people's places", and a man named Parmeet is alive in 2025. Episode 1 does not state the conclusion. Later canon does (Episode 3, per TellyReviews: he boasts of killing the real Parmeet and Dr Tushar).

### B22 · On the road (37:22–38:14) · C
Ananta makes Purvi take off the socks, jacket and muffler she has layered on ("east and west together"). Purvi protests that she will be cold.
Their vehicle dies. There is no one in sight.
- V: a car. Ananta drives, in yellow; Purvi sits beside her. It stops on a dark road through trees and they get out (37:22–38:04).

### B23 · The Naag Mandir (38:14–40:35) · C, TR
A voice welcomes someone: "So you have come where you were meant to come. This is your Naag Mandir and you are the Naagrani of our line. Come and rule from your throne."
